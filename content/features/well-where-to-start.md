### BRIEF HISTORICAL INFORMATION

In the very early 1920's, the Godfrey Phillips tobacco company produced a series of cards to stiffen packets of cigarettes. Initially there were 400 cards which later rose to over 2400, over several years.

Counting error's, team transfer, re print and variations to card presentation there are well over 3000 to collect, some extremely rare and valuable.

Remember, whilst players are shown with differing team names, and also misspelling and

numbering of cards, card re print's, every photograph is the same character, many images from the team groups but others are taken from unknown sources, but possibly are direct lifts from team photograph's of the early 1920's.

### Godfrey Phillips India Ltd was established in

### London in 1844 one of the first

### companies to mass produce and distribute

cigarettes and cigars and still operates to this day.

### Godfrey Phillips Ltd. also produced a large

variety of cigarette cards to stiffen their product boxes, using several topics, such as, film

actresses, animals, aircraft, and famous people from history. The first cards set was launched in 1904 and featured

anonymous pin-up girls.

In the 1920s, the company took advantage of the rise in popularity of association football trading card collecting, releasing football collections in 1920, 1922 and 1923. Godfrey

Phillips issued a series of 1,100 football cards in there first collection, followed by 2,462 in 1922 and 940 in 1923.

Pinnace cards were issued in three sizes, cigarette packet size cards, medium sized in cigar boxes and also postcard sized.

As can be seen from the web site The Pinnace Collection, the amount of cards per box varied and cards could be traded in to obtain the larger cabinet sized

photographs.

Nevertheless, the outbreak of World War II caused a severe shortage of paper and tobacco

companies were forced to cease production of such collections.

It would appear, although its probably coincidental that the initial cards issued in the Packets of

Pinnace cigarettes were inspired by the two teams competing the 1920 FA Cup Final between

Aston Villa, the winners, and runners up Huddersfield Town, the initial 8 cards are taken from those two competing teams, although Smith, integral to everything Huddersfield achieved through the 1920's, had been unavailable due to suspension.
