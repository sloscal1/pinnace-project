This image is not part from the Pinnace Collection but is a Wimbledon team group, dated 1920.

Please note :

Like many minor early 1920's teams included in the Pinnace Collection, some, but not all of the

players are not included in any recorded statistical books or web sites, I found details of Wimbledon players on, www.historicaldons.com.

Unfortunately, the research on this site is a little vague but the owner allowed me to use the information within my project.

I was warned that, much of the research on this site, has come from Genealogy searches and as such much of it is unconfirmed, and should be handled with care.

However, I have included the information available to assist anyone wanting to research the names further. The expanded statistics are thought to be correct, but, must also be handled with care.
