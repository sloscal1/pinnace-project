1883 to 1939

The Casuals Football Club was founded in 1883 until its amalgamation with Corinthians in 1939, its player recruitment restricted to Old Boys from three public schools, Eton, Charterhouse and Westminster. Never members of the league but probably included in the pinnance collection due to their quality status within early organized football, supplying many notable early international players.
