Despite extensive searches and an unanswered E mail to Corinthians Football Club, to trace an historian, I have reluctantly had to concede to defeat and say these players seem to be, and remain…….anonymous.

Its certainly obvious from researching the other players, Nicholas, Morrison, Ashton, Bell, Hegan and Doggart including Bower and the lendendary Howard Baker who are covered in the Chelsea section, these players were all amateurs who occasionally made appearances at professional league clubs, with one or two succumbing to the temptations and lure of being paid for their services, its evident these players were all from 'good stock' and wealthy backgrounds, combining their football with more meaningful careers, interests and standing within society, outside football. The same can be found and probably said with Casuals FC and also to a moderate degree the Civil Service team.
