## Biography

Reid, no relation to James Reid, card 137 page 24, joined Aberdeen for a then club record fee of £1500 and was their top scorer when he joined in 1925

At representative level and whilst playing at Third Lanark, Reid had trials and was selected to play in Home Scots v Anglo Scots and was subsequently selected as an unused reserve for a fixture Scotland V England in 1922. but none of this ever led to a full cap for Scotland.

He did tour South Africa with Third Lanark in the summer of 1923
