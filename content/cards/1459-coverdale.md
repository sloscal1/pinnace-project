## Biography

His father was a marine engineer and as a youngster Coverdale's family re located to Scotland, playing his early football at Rutherglen and Glencairn clubs. In 1912 he moved to Sunderland, who, although not being obliged, compensated his Scottish club paying £10 (£900+ now)
