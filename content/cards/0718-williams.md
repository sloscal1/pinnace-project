## Biography

England 2 appearances first v Ireland 21st October 1922 2-0 win Home International Tournament at The Hawthorns, West Bromwich, the second v Wales 5th March 1923 2-2 draw Home International Tournament Ninian Park, Cardiff, Wales.

Williams was a member of the successful 1926-27 Middlesbrough side, which won the second division championship.
