## Biography

Williams was know as having courage, ability and geniality as he became legendary at Watford. Originally started as a centre forward with Leavesden Hospital, he represented Hertfordshire FA, at county level in both positions. His son Reginald (1922-2011) played for both Watford during WW2, and Chelsea in the 1940's.
