## Biography

* See page 114 Early North American Soccer League article.

Henry Cowan was a Scottish footballer who played for Clyde and Dunfermline Athletic in his native country and for several clubs – primarily the New Bedford Whalers – in the United States, mainly as a full back, later becoming resident in the states. Reports from the time indicate that he was deaf and/or mute.

His son of the same name was born in Scotland but grew up in New Bedford and became an American citizen in order to serve in the US Air Force during World War II; he was killed in action in 1944.
