## Biography

This card character should not be mistaken for the more famous Airdrie player Alexander Reid, 1997-1969. It seems both players were at Airdrieonians at the time of the Pinnace Collection, Alexander Reid, known as 'Alec', left slightly prior to the photographs being compiled.
