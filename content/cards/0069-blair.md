## Biography

Sheffield Wednesday paid £2,000 (£192,000+ today) for his services in 1914

Just prior to the suspension of Football for WW1 Blair sustained a serious injuries in a motor cycle crash, reducing his apps to just 20. Playing for Rangers during WW1, Wednesday found it hard to get him back down to play in England, due largely, to the financial deal they were prepared to pay, so he was transferred to Cardiff.

Cardiff City paid £3,500 (£130,000 + today) for his services in 1920
