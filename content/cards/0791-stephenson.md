## Biography

England 1928 to 1931 3 apps 2 goals first 17th march 1928 a friendly v France, in Paris, 5-1 win Stephenson scoring 2.

Final appearance, March 14th a friendly v France in Paris, 5-2 defeat.

Stephenson unlike his brother Clem Stephenson never really commanded a regular place at Villa, but having said that he did score 22 in 92 appearances and play twice more for England than Clem, before moving on to the clubs mentioned.

He retired in 1937 and took position on the coaching staff at Charlton taking over as manager leading to World War 2.

Following the war he managed Huddersfield.

His son Bob played for Derby and Shrewsbury Town and was a cricketer at Hampshire and Derbyshire between, 1967 and 1980.
