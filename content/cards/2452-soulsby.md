## Biography

Gateshead Rodsley, West Stanley, 1914/15 Newcastle Utd, 1919 South Shields, Darlington, Wallsend, Blyth Spartans, 1922/23 Ashington, Whitburn, Spennymoor Utd, Carlisle Utd .

A difficult character to pinpoint via the internet, with name references varying from James Soulsby to John Norman Soulsby and an alternative to his date of birth, above (ENFA entry), is 6th December 1897, used on Wikipedia and in Michael Joyce's book Football League Players Records 1888 to 1939, but, there is little doubt both sets of details are the same person.

A Wikipedia entry and a record from and the English National Football Archive differ, but details of his playing record appear to have been taken from the ENFA records and I would tend to believe them to be as accurate as anything.

But, these issues aside, his record with Ashington is the more important to this volume and with reference to the Pinnace Collection.

He played 32 times scoring 6 goals
