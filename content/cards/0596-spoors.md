## Biography

Spoors started his career as a Centre Half, but, once at Wednesday, he was converted to either full back position. A determined and consistent player its thought his best years were lost to the war, playing only one more season after the conclusion of the conflict and moving onto Barnsley in 1920 for two seasons.
