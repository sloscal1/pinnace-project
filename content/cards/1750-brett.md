## Biography

Frank joined Aston Villa as an amateur but Villa did not sign him professionally. He left them for Redditch joining as a semi-professional, whilst still working at Cadburys, in the chocolate factory.

Manchester united showed interest signing him for £300 (£12,000+ now), a dispute ensuing, Villa claiming he was still a their player. The F.A. intervened and fined United £5.5shillings (.£207+ now).

Frank played only 10 times, before returning to Villa in a deal which saw Frank Barson (see page 10), travel in the opposite direction. During WW1 he was a rear gunner for the Royal Artillery, and much later, in life following the end of WW2 he ran a coal merchants and latterly a bookmakers.
