## Honours

- Glasgow Celtic : Scottish League Champions 1916/17

## Biography

O'Kane was somewhat an early football journeyman.

Without ever establishing himself as a first choice at Glasgow Celtic and scoring only 20 goals in 14 appearances, along the way he assisted the club to two league championships, a Glasgow Charity Merchants final win and a scoring appearance in a Glasgow Cup semi final.

During WW1 he served with the Royal Irish Regiment and the Royal Engineers.

Stockport County : Division 3 North Champions 1921/22
