## Biography

A speedy and skillful winger able to play either side of the park Harrold had three seasons in Birmingham and District leagues before joining Leicester Fosse during the final full season prior to World War One.

When normal life resumed following the war he made 18 appearances scoring twice before joining Nottingham Forest in May 1920, enjoying a good season being second highest scorer with 8 goals. The following season during Forests promotion from Division two he played another 13 times but losing his position to Syd Gibson (card 1705) who came in from Kettering Town.

Being recommended by Pat Nellis (card 1706) he joined Accrington Stanley moving there during the latter stages of 1922/23 season.

Sadly things didn't go well making only 6 appearances whilst scoring one goal before sustaining a serious ankle injury whilst playing for Stanley against Durham City which effectively finished his career.

A benefit game was set up for him from teams comprising of Forest & Leicester Fosse players versus a Lancashire/Yorkshire select side, 3,000 attending the game.

Following his career, his injury did not prevent him pursuing his other sporting interest of Cricket, with Newton Wednesbury, he also became a licensee in the Wednesbury area.
