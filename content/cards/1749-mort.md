## Biography

England 1924-1926 3 apps 0 goals first app 3 march 1924 v Wales Ewood Park Blackburn 2-1 defeat (Clem Stephenson also played) last app 17th April 1926 v Scotland Old Trafford Manchester 1-0 defeat (Billy Walker also played) both games Home International Tournament.

Tommy Weston ( card No3) in the Villa team becoming a great crowd favorite and forming a partnership with Tommy Smart (card 674) the fans calling them 'Death or Glory' duo.

Not the tallest Mort started as a part timer at Altrincham moving to Rochdale as full time player in June 1921.

Mort replaced but at 12stone was obviously stocky and strong, he was a great exponent of the slide tackle.

Retiring in 1935, he returned home and became a business man in Wigan.
