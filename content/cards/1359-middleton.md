## Biography

Middleton served as a private in the Football Battalion (Macrae's) of the Middlesex Regiment during the First World War.

There is a suggestion that during 1912 he played 34 times for Crystal Palace, also turning out for Birmingham and Folkstone but these details cannot be qualified.
