## Biography

Another Hearts player who played for them throughout WW1, and another member of McCrae's Battalion (see page 127).

Whilst still at war, despite being an Hearts player he appeared as a guest for Leeds City in 1916 not making any appearances.

While several of his teammates fought and perished at the Battle of the Somme, a problematic shoulder meant that Wilson could not fire a rifle effectively, and he was held on reserve. He did see frontline action in 1917 at Arras and was wounded. At the conclusion of the hostilities in 1918 Wilson returned to Edinburgh and was in the Hearts side which reached but lost the final of the 1919 Victory Cup
