## Biography

Tom Eatock was a renown and accomplished sprinter, which assisted in leaving many a full back standing in his wake, but he played in a Wigan Borough team that lacked success and after two seasons he transferred to Bolton Wanderers, where he found himself playing understudy to the accomplished Ted Vizard in a season where Bolton finished third in the First Division.

In 1926 he left the professional game turning to the licensing trade as a form of income and to his first love athletics.
