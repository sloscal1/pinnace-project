## Honours

- Tottenham Hotspur : Football League Second Division: 1919/20. FA Charity Shield: 1921.

## Biography

England 2 appearance 1921-22, the first v Scotland 9th April 1921 3-0 defeat, Hampden Park, Glasgow, Home International Championship. His second and last v Wales 13th March 1922, 1-0 win at Anfield, Liverpool. Both in the Home International Championship.

Smith finished his career at Spurs in May 1931 when he joined Northfleet United F.C. a club affiliated with Tottenham, as a coach. In later years he went on to be coach/trainer at Young Boys in Switzerland, Harwich and Parkeston, Hitchin and Stevenage Borough.
