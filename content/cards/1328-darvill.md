| 1924-1925 | Watford | 1 | 0 | Berkhamsted, Hertfordshire born centre forward Alfred Darvill was one of a family of three local footballing brothers, his older brother Harvey played 75 times for Fulham. Alfred was playing for Watford Corinthians when he joined Ilford in October 1920. In June 1923 he joined Division Three (South) Watford as an amateur, turning professional in September 1924, making his only appearance for their first eleven in a 4-0 defeat at Exeter City in February 1925, a few weeks after his brother Harvey had tragically died as a result of injury sustained in a collision with Leicester City's goalkeeper while playing for Fulham. Alfred subsequently had a trial with Ebbw Vale in October 1925. With thanks to vintagefootballers.com |
