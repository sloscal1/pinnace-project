## Biography

An Inside right or half back, Muirhead, could and did, play anywhere in the forward or half back line. He started in Fife Junior football, at Hearts of Beath FC, Cowdenbeath, but this was interrupted during WW1 where he worked for the army pay corps. While still serving he joined Hibernian moving on to Rangers, in Glasgow in 1917. There were few opportunities, at first, so took up an army commission and was demobbed in 1919, still only 22. He played a key role in a Rangers team that dominated Scottish football for the next decade, winning 8 league medals, interrupted for six months, by a stint in a America, as player manager of Boston Soccer Club, despite that and resisting offers from other big clubs he remained loyal to Rangers. Perhaps, his only regret is not winning the Scottish cup being injured for the victory of 1928 and 1930, and lost the finals of 1922 and 1929.

As an International he made 8 appearances, captining the team in two, but was never able to establish himself as a regular choice.

Retiring in 1930 he took up a post writing for the Evening Citizen Newspaper, and, gave a try to management, firstly at St. Johnstone 1931 to 1936 and then, Preston North End 1936/37 season. At this point he surprised the football world, by returning to journalism, writing for the Scottish Daily Express where he later became sports editor.
