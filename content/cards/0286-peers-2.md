## Biography

Peers started his footballing life as an outside right then, switching to full back, before finding success between the posts.

Peers became the first Port Vale player to be Internationally capped making 4 appearances whilst at the club, the remainder whilst at Wolves. Apparently, he would have likely played more regularly for his country, whilst at Vale, had it not been for the clubs reluctancy to allow him leave, to play.

At Wolves he became a regular until Noel George* (1897-1929) temporarily replace him in 1921 thus missing out on an FA cup final appearance.

Peers played for several clubs during WW1 including Stoke and Walsall.

He finished his career and had three seasons at Hednesford Town before becoming a public house licensee, in the Wolverhampton area. * Noel George died, whilst a player, at the age of 32 from a disease in his gums, thought to have resulted from ill fitting dentures.
