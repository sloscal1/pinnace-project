## Biography

This is a card/image that creates one of those anomalies relating to set reprints over a period of years, it is something that the reader/enthusiast must make their own minds up about, but, certainly there are not two players featured in the collection called James at the time of photographing, certainly Griffith James who played at that time was not included in this set.

Aberdare born outside right David "Dai" James joined his hometown Third Division (South) club Aberdare Athletic in 1921 making his Football League debut for them at Queen's Park Rangers in November the same year, scoring both goals in a 2-0 win over Luton Town in May 1922 as he made just 6 appearances during his debut season. A regular from the end of October 1922, he missed just two matches a season in 1923-24 and 1925-26, and was an ever present for Aberdare in 1924-25.

In 1926 after 16 goals in 185 appearances for Aberdare, he was sold to Brighton & Hove Albion also in the Third Division south scoring 9 goals in 32 appearances for The Seagulls. To his last appearance for them in April 1929, all but four of his appearances for Brighton came in their 1927-28 campaign after he broke into their eleven in October 1927, helping Brighton finish fourth in the Third Division (South). It is probable he was forced to retire through injury, although his latter years of playing are none existent .

NB : There is much confusion over this card image as to the true identity of the character shown

It is possible, after inconclusive photo comparisons that this image, published in 1922 and purporting to show G. James, is not Dai James but Griffith James.

Griffith James was born at Cwmaman, near Aberdare, 2th March 1899 to 1978 and was a goalkeeper. (the player in photograph is not wearing a goalkeeping shirt), who made 16 appearances for Aberdare between October 1923 and May 1924. The possible goalkeeper in this collection group appears to be E. Leahy, card 939. The fact that Dai James was a first team regular for Aberdare in 1922 suggests it is far more likely to be him, than a player who wasn't in the first team picture at all in 1922/23. The Aberdare team group image from 1924/25 in the Vintage Footballers library shows Dai James, but the quality isn't high and although there are similarities, it is somewhat inconclusive, but it is fair to assume that this image in the collection is in fact Dai James and not Griffith James.

Research on Griffith James reveals little or nothing concerning a player of this name or with these personnel details available that fully clarify who is who. The logical answer is that the image is most likely to be David 'Dai' James.
