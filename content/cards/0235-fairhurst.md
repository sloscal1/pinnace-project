## Biography

Playing his early football at Darwen, he joined Preston North End in 1912 but, made no appearances for the Lilywhites. Whilst a mild mannered level headed family man, away from football he was a strong, stocky, tough tackling defender who put fear into opposition forwards making a fine understanding with Bert Tulloch in defence, together forming a strong defensive partnership that coincided with a successful period in Blackpool's fortunes.

However, tragedy struck when on Boxing day 1921 in a game v Barnsley he was stretchered off unconscious following a particularly strong tackle with an opposition player, knocking him unconscious. Despite treatment, Fairhurst never regained consciousness and died at his home resulting from his injury.
