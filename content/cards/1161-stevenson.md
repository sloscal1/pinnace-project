## Biography

Described as a sturdy left wingman, his skill, speed and footwork forecast a bright future, endorsed by some big money moves, £1000 ( £51,000+ now) to Middlesbrough, £750 ( £38,000+ now) to Mid Rhondda and £600 ( £30,000+ now) to Sheffield United and £400 ( £22,000+ now) to Wigan Borough, but he failed to realise his full potential.
