## Biography

Falkirk's regular penalty taker for much of his time there (anecdotally it is reported that when such a kick was awarded he would jog up from his defensive position to the penalty spot and strike the ball at goal without pausing.

In 1927 toured North America with Scottish FA party, but never had a full Scotland cap. Scott was an accomplished golfer.
