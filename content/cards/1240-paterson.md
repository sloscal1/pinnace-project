## Biography

A poorly referenced player, who, according to Ironsides Who's Who of Newport County, was a prolific scorer during 1922-23 season, but with the reserves, scoring 30 goals including five hat-tricks, but not making any appearances for the first team, which is at odds with information available on ENFA (as above)

He was once selected to play for the Welsh League representative team but Newport would not release him as they needed his services at club level.
