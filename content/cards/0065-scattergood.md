## Biography

England 1 app 0 goals v Wales 17th March 1913 4-3 win Home Championship at Ashton Gate, Bristol, England.

Scattergood was one of a long string of early Derby County keepers who were local lads.

His difference was, he only stood 5' 8". Obviously, viewed then as small, for keeper, as opposed to the giants that play now.

Was a regular in Derby's 1911-12 Second Division winning team, played once for England and survived WW1 despite being gassed.

His prowess has a goal scorer resulted as being penalty taker for both of his clubs, trusted more because of a strong shot, rather than his technique, he continued as penalty taker at Bradford until in one game, South Shields keeper saved one and Scattergood had to show his prowess as a sprinter, to get back to his goal line.

His son Kenneth Scattergood 1912-1988 was also a professional footballer playing in goal for Sheff. Wed. Wolves, Bristol City, Stoke City, & Derby Co between1931-1936.
