## Biography

Lockton was a student at University in London and was on Fulham's books as an amateur when he transferred to Nottingham Forest. On News Year Day 1914 he played for London Amateurs v Southern Counties and only a matter of days after signing for Forest played for the South against the North in a trial for England Amateurs, played at Oxford.

Sadly he didn't impact well with Forest, failing to assist with goals, as Forest had to re apply for Division two status. Seemingly he did not return to Forest after WW1. Instead he became a school master and taught at St Dunstan's College in Catford for 38 years before retiring in 1952. It does appear that he had a later association with Crystal Palace but no records exist to show he actually played for them, similarly there are no records available concerning his time at Casuals.

He was also a useful cricketer, playing for Surrey between 1919 and 1926.
