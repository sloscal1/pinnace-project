## Honours

- Manchester City: FA Cup finalist: 1926

## Biography

On retirement from the game he became licensee of the Royal Albert Hotel, Southampton turning out for two seasons on a part-time basis for Guildford City. He move to Eastleigh in 1941 to run the Cricketers Arms where he remained until his death in 1950, aged 58.
