## Biography

This strong, tough tackling full back joined the Hammers from Bradford City following spells with both Rochdale and Castleford Town in May 1921, making his debut against South Shields partnering Billy Cope (1884-1937).

Recovering from a serious injury incurred during the next campaign Jack settled down to become a regular in the hammers first team and was appointed captain for a period of his tenure at the club.

During 1927 he left for Fulham in a deal with fellow hammer George Horler (1895-1967) for a combined fee of £850 ( £45,000+ now).

Staying only a short time he joined local newly formed team Thames, who actually played at West Ham's old ground in Prince Regents Lane, London.

Following financial difficulties Thames folded in 1933 and Jack joined several other clubs nine of which he made any real lasting impact with.

With a faltering career, Jack found employment with local firm Dorman Long where he continued to play as a permit player.
