## Biography

He represented Scottish XI team on one occasion.

Dunn came to Everton just prior to the arrival of Henry Ritchie (Card 1285 see above) in 1928, both added to the Everton team sporting Dixie Dean who had completed his record scoring 60 goals in a season, the season before.

They were expected to add creation, to compliment Deans goal scoring, but whilst Ritchie soon returned to Scotland, Dunn was somewhat a success at the club, he would win the league with Everton in1931-32 season, and the FA cup in 1930 and assisting Everton's return to the top Division in 1930-31 season.
