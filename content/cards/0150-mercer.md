## Biography

Mercer transferred to Sheffield Utd for a then record fee of £4,500 (£162,000+ now) he represented the Football League XI and won two caps for England, scoring once, in a 6–1 victory over Belgium on 19 March 1923. Mercer was the older brother of fellow footballer Arthur Mercer (1902-1994) and the pair briefly played together at Sheffield Utd. Mercer's other brother Richard was an amateur footballer and his son David (1918-1986) also became a professional footballer, he is not related to William Henry Mercer (Card 616 in the collection). In 1939 he was working as a golf club groundsman. He once scored six goals in a league game, only for the match to be abandoned !
