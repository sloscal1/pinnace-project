## Biography

At the outset of his career, when playing mainly in reserve team football, the slightly built wingman saw his Bradford Park Avenue team, suffer successive relegations from Division one in 1920/21 season, to Division two a year later.

Moving onto Stockport County for season 1922/23 he was a key figure, in their narrow escape from relegation from Division two.

After the opening six games of the following campaign Humphrey lost his place in the first team, reappearing just once in an FA cup game versus Norwich City, another defeat.

Joining Nelson the following season an exchange deal involving Bob Hutchinson (see Card 1858, Page 585), he was again involved in desperate scraps to avoid relegation, only appearing eight times during that season, as Nelsons sojourn in Division two ended.

Humprhey drifted away from league football into none league, with Selby Town.
