## Biography

Hoad was born in Eltham, London, on 27 December 1890. His father, Joseph, was a greyhound trainer. While playing as an amateur for Blackpool and Manchester City, he trained as a lawyer but did not pursue a career in law, instead progressing to professional football. Football was not a great source of income at this time and Hoad worked as a Court Writer and as a Commercial Traveller. He was married and had one daughter. Hoad served as a private in the Army Service Corps during the First World War.

Described as player of ripe experience at 32 years of age, giving Nelson good service, despite arriving late in life.

Observors of the game, from that time, said he had 'very smart footwork and centered the ball splendidly'. One observer rated him 'one of the fastest wingers in the third division', drawing jovial comparisons to his father's skill of breeding greyhounds for racing!

Nelson : 3rd Division North Champions 1923.

Thanks to Garth Dykes, author of Nelson in the Football League, a complete record and Who's who, for much of the finer information.
