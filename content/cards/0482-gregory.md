## Biography

Known as the 'Express Man' and 'Greg', ginger haired Howard Gregory was a fine left winger, quick witted fast and plucky who teamed up well with Fred Morris, immediately after WW1.

He was the older brother of Clarence Gregory (1900-1975), who played League football for Sunderland and Queen's Park Rangers, and John 'Jack' Gregory (?) of .Queen's Park Rangers.
