## Biography

Scraggs was a highly rated and decorated player within Irish Football, winning multi times, differing Irish league competition medals with Glentoran, at a time when Glentoran were the dominant Irish league team in Belfast.

Appearing 7 times for the Irish league XI he never actually played in a winning team. Retiring in 1928, he was already 40 years of age and had made, not including his time in Scotland, 305 appearances scoring 18 goals for Glentoran.
