## Biography

Described as a 'Tip top inside forward' whilst at Airdrie, injury issues, sustained, whist at Fulham, would plague his career, leading to missing two Cup Finals whilst at Celtic. He left Celtic in 1928 following a dispute over wages.

Following this date his career is a little vague but he did, retire in 1933 to pursue interests in a family shop business, believed to be a gentlemen's outfitters in Glasgow.
