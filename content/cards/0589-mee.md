## Biography

NB: Mee was inducted into the Hall of Fame at Bloomfield Road, when it was officially opened by former Blackpool player Jimmy Armfield in April 2006. Organised by the Blackpool Supporters Association, Blackpool fans around the world voted on their all-time heroes. Five players from each decade are inducted; Mee is in the pre-1950s. After retiring, Mee bought a pub in Blackpool. He died in July 1978 in Poulton-le-Fylde.
