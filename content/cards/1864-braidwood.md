## Biography

In the 1920's Footballers standing at 6 feet or more were rare, and in Ernie's days at Oldham the 1920's sports cartoonist ASM portrayed him like a skyscraper. Early days at Oldham went well until a change of manager effected his regular selection. In the close season of 1922 he moved to Nelson managed by Nelson player manager and a former team mate, Dave Wilson, described as a splendid reliable centre back ideally built for the job he rarely had an off day and became a member of the 1923 Championship winning team, and runners up two seasons later, recorded maximum appearances in both campaigns. He came close again with Rochdale in 1926 who were runners up to stoke city. By trade Ernie was a bricklayer
