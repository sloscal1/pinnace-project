## Biography

Wright started his career in football at the age of 16 probably playing for Aberdeen Boys and Aberdeen East End, and graduating into the Aberdeen league side but these dates are not readily available.

He stayed at the club captaining the team in his latter years at the club, before Hearts came in a paid the sum £2500 (£121,000+ now) for his services a then record transfer fee received by Aberdeen.

During WW1 Wright served with both the Gordon Highlanders and the Royal Engineers.

Whilst serving he played for Millwall as a guest.

In 1927 he moved to Morton for a fee of £800 (£42,000+ now).

At the age of 31 he was interviewed and became first Secretary/Manager of Dumfries club, Queen of the South, also signing himself as a player.
