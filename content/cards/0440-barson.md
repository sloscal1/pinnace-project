## Biography

England 1 app v Wales March 15th 1920 2-1 defeat at Highbury London.

Born in Sheffield one of six sons, Barson, working as a steel hoop maker, played his early football for amateur sides like Albion FC and Cammell Laird's before joining Barnsley in 1911

He moved to Villa in October 1919 for £2800, winning the cup with them against Huddersfield, at Stamford Bridge in 1920.

In 1922 he moved on to Manchester Utd for fee reported to be £5000, following disagreements with the Villa board over where he should reside, favouring to stay in Sheffield rather than live in Birmingham.

A tough tackler and exponent of the shoulder charge he was perhaps viewed as the Vinnie Jones of that era as he was hated by opposition fans, feared by opposition players and often had to be escorted from the field of play to protect against aggressive fans. In one violent encounter against Fulham he received a seven month ban from the game for an injury inflicted to a player.

Joining Rhyl as a player manager he stayed there until his contract was terminated in 1935 later that year joining Stourbridge, briefly, as manager before returning to Villa as a coach staying until the outbreak of World War Two.

Following the war he coached at Swansea Town, and had two seasons coaching Lye Town.
