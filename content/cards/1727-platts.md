## Biography

Anston born winger Bob Platts joined Second Division Notts County from Anston United in 1920 making his first team debut at Bury in May 1921. He was never a regular at Meadow Lane managing a best of 18 appearances in 1923-24 but he did manage to score three times in his 53 appearances over his 4 year spell, which ended in January 1925 before he was released, moving to Southend United that summer but failing to make any first team appearances for The Shrimpers, before joining non league Heanor Town in 1927.
