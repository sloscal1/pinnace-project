## Biography

Between 1906 and 1913 with the exception of one season of league football with Gainsborough Trinity, Smelt played for many junior and unaffiliated teams including Rotherham Main Institute, Rotherham County twice, Sutton Junction, Rotherham Town, Masbrough Thursday, and Chesterfield.

On leaving Barrow at the end of 1926/27 season he returned to amateur football turning out for Frickley Colliery, Scunthorpe & Lindsay Utd as a trialist and latterly Hurst.
