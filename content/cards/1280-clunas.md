## Representative

- 1924-1925 Scotland. First appearance v England on 12 April 1924 in a 1–1 draw at The Wembley Stadium. Second v Wales in a 3–0 win, on 31 October 1925, scoring the third goal.

## Biography

Clunas the son of a Scottish Police Inspector, was described as an imposing half back with a fine blend of strength and artistry.

At Sunderland, Raich Carter (1913-1994), highlighted him, as being the best penalty taker he had ever seen. He later coached Swiss side Basel, before returning to coach and play for Inverness Thistle, taking him to reirement in 1937.
