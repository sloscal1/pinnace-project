## Honours

- Div one champions, 1930/31, 1932/33 FA cup winner 1920, runner up 1924

## Biography

Aston Villa 1919-1933 478 apps 214 goals

England 1920-1932 18 apps 9 goals.

First app v Ireland October 23rd 1920 2-0 win Roker park Sunderland

Last app v Austria friendly Dec 7th 1932 4-3 win Stamford Bridge London.

Teams managed 1933-37 Sheffield Wednesday 1938 Chelmsford City 1939-60 Nottingham Forest As a manager : Sheffield Wednesday. FA cup winner 1935, FA charity shield 1935.

Nottingham Forest. FA cup 1959, FA charity shield 1959, Second Division runners up 1956/57 Walker was from a footballing family, father George Walker, 1877-1930, played for Crystal Palace in two brief spells between 1905 and 1909.

Young Billy went on to phenomenal success as a one club player and later outstanding legendary manager.

His overall appearance record is 531 between 1920 and 1934 scoring 244 goals, incredibly 214 coming in 478 games.

To this day he remains Villa's all time to scorer.

Once asked, former club mate, Tommy Smart, Card 674, " what's the team today Tommy? " he replied " Billy Walker and 10 others "

This character is a true legend of football and is certainly worth further investigation by the enthusiast football fan, See recent book 'Once, Twice, Three times a winner' by Edward Giles and his own autobiography 'Soccer in the blood' a 1960's publication.
