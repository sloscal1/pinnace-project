## Biography

Billy Palmer played for eight different clubs in over a seventeen-year span that was interrupted by the First World War.

In 1912 he joined Southern League club Bristol Rovers, where he earned the nickname "Lady Palmer" because he rarely dirtied his shorts during a match. His forty-six league and cup appearances for Rovers, plus 11 goals, were of sufficient quality for Everton to pay the Bristol club £800 (£64,000+ today) for his services, then a very large fee, and to take him for a second spell in Division One of the Football League. His time at Everton ended when League football was suspended due to the First World War, after having made 23 appearances, scoring one goal, in the First Division.
