## Biography

Charlton Ath 24 apps 1 goal

1922 Aberaman Ath

After a season with Welsh side Aberaman Athletic from June 1922, Wilson returned to Belfast to play with Linfield in June 1923. Although the major honours eluded him during his stay at Windsor Park, Wilson was selected three times for the Irish League between 1923 and 1925, and twice, for Ireland, winning his first cap against South Africa at Belfast in September 1924 and his second and final cap against Wales at The Racecourse Ground in Wrexham in April 1925. He finished his career with Larne, in 1925.
