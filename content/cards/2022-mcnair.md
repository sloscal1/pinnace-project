## Biography

It would seem, McNair escaped Scottish football, for the financial attraction of the first American Soccer League (ASL), as he is listed as playing for Springfield Babes. They folded as a team at the end of 1926, but Clamdiggers were renamed Provident Gold Bugs during 1928/29 season and won the American Soccer Association Cup in 1929. In 1930 they were bought up and became Fall River FC. It is not known if McNair had a long association with these clubs, or if in fact he stayed in America following his retirement from playing, but perhaps the fact he is not documented anywhere historically in the UK football archive is perhaps indicative that he actually emigrated fully to the States and stayed there until his death.
