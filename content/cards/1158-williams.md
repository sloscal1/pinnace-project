## Biography

Williams was a small but sturdy, wing half joining Borough after 2 seasons of reserve football at Everton.

Said to be light haired and light hearted, he was a popular and gentlemanly player. A strong tackler and expert header of the ball he possessed unusual stamina, which made him difficult to beat.

He was regular between 1921 and 1921 being invited back to the club for season following a stint at his home town club of Holyhead.

He represented the North Wales FA in 1924, in a game against the Scottish Junior FA.
