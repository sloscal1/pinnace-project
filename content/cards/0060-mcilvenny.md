## Biography

Started his life in football, playing for north east teams, Willington Athletic and South Shields, moving to Bradford City in 1911. Initially, his impact as a goal scorer was moderate, but stopping at Bradford during WW1 his prowess as a goal getter increased and also continued post war, his first season after the war being his most productive. He was given a benefit match in February 1921 against West Brom. In 1922 he was sold to Blackpool. Whilst his scoring stats at City show only pre and post war stats, his goal tally (with wartime included), shows a combined goal tally of 91. He retired at the end of the 1923 season.

His son Harold Jowett 'Harry' McIlvenny,1922-2009, was also a professional footballer with Bradford Pak Avenue and was also a Centre Forward.
