## Biography

Right back Tom Brandon Jr was the son of William Thomas 'Tom' Brandon, (1867-1941) of Blackburn Rovers and Scotland, and also the nephew of Henry 'Harry' Brandon, (1870-1935), who won the FA Cup with Sheffield Wednesday in 1896. His father Tom Brandon Snr played for St Mirren, Blackburn Rovers and Sheffield Wednesday in the 1890's, his uncle Harry Brandon played for St Mirren, Clyde, Sheffield Wednesday and Chesterfield in the same decade.
