## Biography

Loughborough Corinthians

Despite losing 4 years to the war Belton registered an amazing amount of appearances and consistency each season.

Press of the day described his play as, "plays a strong defensive game, and lacks nothing in determination and courage" Belton served as a private in the Leicestershire Regiment and the Labour Corps during the First World War. He was wounded at the Battle of the Somme in 1916 and was evacuated to Britain
