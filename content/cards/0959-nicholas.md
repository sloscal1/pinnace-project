## Biography

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia born outside right Frederick Nicholas attended Hertford College, Oxford University. During the War Nicholas served with the 1st Bedfordshire Regiment. Attaining the rank of Colonel and was awarded the Military Cross. He appeared for The Corinthians in the early and mid 1920's, also being a member of their European tour parties.

He was first capped by England Amateurs in January 1920 when he scored in a 9-0 win over Wales. He won 4 caps in total, all won during 1920, scoring three goals. He also played for Great Britain in the 1920 Summer Olympics in Antwerp, Belgium, scoring as Britain were beaten 3-1 by Norway. He also played county cricket for Essex and captained the county side into the late 1920's, averaging 26.34 with the bat from 76 first class matches with a high score of 140. He also did athletics and played hockey to a high level. He is the grandfather of former Hampshire and England 'A' cricketer and television commentator Mark Nicholas.
