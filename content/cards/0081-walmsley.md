## Biography

Albert Walmsley was born in Blackburn and played in local junior football before joining Darwen. He remained with Darwen for two seasons before signing for Blackburn Rovers in 1907. It is said, Walmsley made little impact on his new club immediately and had to bide his time in the reserves before first team opportunity came along, despite playing 28 times scoring twice in 1907/08 season. However, after establishing himself in the first team his career never looked back. Not a spectacular wing half particularly compared to William Bradshaw (1884-?) who he often partnered, Walmsley was still a tireless worker whose outstanding characteristic was his consistency. A member of the both the 1911 and 1913 league winning sides Walmsley was another player whose career was badly effected by war. Although he returned to Blackburn Rovers, to play in the first season after the war, he was no longer the player he had been and Rovers allowed him to move to Stockport County.

It was thought this player is actually Richard Walmsley, born 1894, Blackburn Rovers 1919-1922, but its been pointed out that neither Albert or Richard were ever included in the Pinnace Collection for Blackburn Rovers, together, and that neither were related. Card 81 is definitely, Albert.
