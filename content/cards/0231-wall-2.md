## Honours

- Manchester United Division One Champions 1907-08 & 1910-11. FA Cup winner 1909.

## Biography

His playing record shows a loan to Cowdenbeath in 1916 but no record exists as to how often he played for them, its thought that he played, as a guest, as in 1919 he joined Oldham for a £900 (£ 38,000+ now) fee, surely indicating he was still on United's books, until the end of WW1.

International : 7 full caps 1907-1913. the first, March 18th 1907 v Wales at Craven Cottage, London a 1-1 draw and his last February 15th v Ireland Windsor Park Belfast, 2-1 defeat. He also appeared on 4 between occasions for a League XI between 1910-12.
