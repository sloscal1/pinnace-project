## Biography

McLachlan was a Scottish professional footballer and manager. He played for a number of amateur sides as a youth but began his professional career with Clyde in the Scottish Football League. He signed for Football League First Division side Cardiff City in 1925 for a fee of £2,000 (£84,000+ present day). He made more than 150 appearances for the side over four seasons in all competitions and was part of the team that won the 1927 FA Cup Final.

In 1929, he was sold to Manchester United where he was later appointed club captain. He later played for Chester before being appointed manager of French side Le Havre in 1934, where he also briefly played. He returned to Scotland in 1935, becoming manager of Queen of the South. He spent two seasons in charge of the side and also led the team on a 1936 overseas tour of France. He left the club in March 1937 and took up employment as a draughtsman before emigrating to the United States. Honours: Cardiff City: FA Cup: 1927, Charity Shield: 1927.
