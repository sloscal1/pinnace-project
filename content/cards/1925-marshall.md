## Biography

Marshall was a player of average skill, with an unusual knock kneed gait, and not robustly built. What he lacked in that area of defensive play, he made up for, by is tricky thinking game. In 1923 he suffered a serious breakdown in health and despite and operation this failed to save his life, dying in June 1923 at the age of 35.
