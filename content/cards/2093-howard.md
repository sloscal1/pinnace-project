## Biography

A really difficult profile to track with conflicting information in differing places on the internet and in record books.

Above is as close as I can get,

It seems, his real name is Julian Frederick Howard, but was always known as 'Fred'.

It appears following junior football with a local team he signed for Manchester City his career setting off at a fine pace until the advent of WW1 where he seemingly stayed, playing several more games during the war period for City, but on resumption of full time football he quickly moved on to several other clubs without settling anywhere and without making any impact, including at Clyde.

An interesting fact about Howard is that when debuting for Manchester City in January 1913 he scored 4 times, the first three in the first 13 minutes and the fourth after 20. Liverpool's goalkeeper that day was International Kenny Campbell. By coincidence on Howards debut at New Brighton, a friendly against Stoke City, Stoke's goalkeeper, that day, was again, Campbell, but unfortunately he did not score on this occasion.
