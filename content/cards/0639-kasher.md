## Biography

Sturdy and well built, Joe played his early football in the North Eastern amateur leagues, with WW1 looming, he joined the Royal Navy, playing regularly for his unit He would be drafted to France where he was subsequently captured and became a POW for two years.

Returning to England following the end of the conflict he signed professionally for Sunderland playing in 90 league and cup games for the North East giants before Stoke paid £1,200 ( £57,000+ now) for his services in 1922. Strong in air, Joe had retained his military heir, dominant in marshalling his defensive line.

In 1924 after failing to agree a new contract at Sunderland he returned home Willington, where he played part time for Carlisle in the North Eastern amateur league before signing professionally for Accrington Stanley, who paid £1,000 ( £50,000+ now) for his service, Stoke, presumably still having retained his registration as their player.

Making 49 league and cup appearances for the Lancashire club over two years, he would retire and take over the Park Hotel, next door to Accrington's ground. Joe died in 1992 aged 98 yrs Thanks to 'Stoke City Who's who' by Tony Matthews
