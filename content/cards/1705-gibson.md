## Biography

Fair haired Sid Gibson Joined Forest for a £500 (£20,000+ now) fee, from non-league football, with bigger clubs, notably Aston Villa and Everton seeking his services. He gave Forest great service playing 276 times winning the Division Two championship in in 1921/22 season before moving onto Sheffield United for a then record, for both clubs, fee, of £5,000 (£259,000+ now), in 1928.

Retiring during 1933 he joined Southend United as assistant manager until 1935 when he commenced scouting duties at West Ham. He was an exceptionally talented church organist and was able to play any popular tune on request.

Described as, exceptionally fast and tricky, with a strong shot, he was forced into retirement with knee injuries at the age of 33 receiving a £350 (£20,000+ now) compensation from the FA. He took part in exhibition games there after, along with the famous David Bone Jack (1898-1958), he also the subject of a record five figure fee in 1928, Arsenal paying £10,000 for his services from Bolton Wanderers (The first five figure transfer in professional football).
