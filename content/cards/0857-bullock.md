## Biography

Played Army football from 1912 until the end of WW1. Served with 2nd Battalion East Yorkshire Reg in the France, Balkans and Middle East. Received an army pension for deafness, hereditary, but exacerbated by the War.

Ended his career with two seasons at Runcorn in the Cheshire leagues.
