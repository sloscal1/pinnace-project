## Biography

Played for Scottish XI May 1920

McLachlan joined Vila from St Cuthberts Wanderers in 1913.

Playing only three times for Villa he moved back to Scotland to play for Aberdeen in 1914.

Serving as a gunner during WW1 he returned to Aberdeen in 1919 being made team captain.

Following a career threatening groin injury in 1927 he did return to play, but he decided to leave and joined Hearts.

He left for Hearts in 1927 playing later in an unknown role for Elgin City.

His brothers Fred and John both played football for Partick Thistle, Dundee and Aston Villa..
