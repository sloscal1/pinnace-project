## Biography

Page was born in Liverpool. His father worked as a crane driver at the nearby docks. His parents had ten children, although five died. Three of his brothers William Page 1896-1981, Thomas Page 1888-1973 and Louis Antonio Page 1899-1959 also went on to become professional footballers.
