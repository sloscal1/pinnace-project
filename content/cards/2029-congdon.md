## Biography

Cornish winger Jim Congdon played for Exeter City on half-a-dozen occasions in the early 1920s. He joined the club from Millbrook Rangers in East Cornwall and later played for Accrington Stanley and Torquay United.

Son of a farm laborer, Jim made a favorable impression when playing for Exeter City in pre-season practice matches in 1921. He had been playing for Millbrook Rangers and had represented Cornwall and the Plymouth & District League before first appearing for City's reserve team towards the end of the 1920/21 season. Signing as a professional, his Football League debut came in a 1-0 victory at Southend United in October 1921.

Playing just five more games, Jim left St James' Park after a season and signed for Accrington Stanley in the summer of 1922. Having made five outings in Third Division North, he returned to Devon, signing for Torquay United in March 1923.
