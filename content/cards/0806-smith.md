## Biography

Smith started his football career in the town of his birth with Kettering Town, before Watford paid a fee of £125 (£4,000+ now) for his services in March 1921. He remained at the Hertfordshire-based club for the rest of his career, making 341 appearances in all competitions.

In 1925, Smith played in a representative match in London where experiments took place to change the offside law.

Following his retirement from football, in 1932, he remained in Hertfordshire, and became a bus driver.

He died in July 1988 in St Albans, aged 90.
