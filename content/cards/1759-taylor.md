## Biography

NB : A possible anomaly occurs with this card.

It appears that the card is possibly a misspell, which does occur on many in the set.

According to research points available, there is no W. Taylor associated with Rochdale, at all, and particularly during the relevant times that these cards were printed and reprinted.

However, the player, Frederick Taylor is referenced on a Wikipedia entry as playing for Chelsea to 1917 and Brentford from 1919 to 1920 and Rochdale,19 times during 1921 (the relevant times, these images were being collated). So there are some vague references in terms of gaps in the time scape of the post WW1 period.

Unfortunately, Wikipedia is the only point of reference as more solid avenues of research do not mention Frederick Taylor playing at Rochdale.

My own opinion is, that the coincidence that W. Taylor and Frederick Taylor are, one and the same, is too strong to ignore.
