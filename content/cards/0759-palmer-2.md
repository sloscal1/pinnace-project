## Biography

In 1912 he joined Southern League club Bristol Rovers, where he earned the nickname "Lady Palmer" because he rarely dirtied his shorts during a match. His appearances for Rovers were of sufficient quality for Everton to pay the Bristol club £800 (£70,000+ today) for his services, then a very large fee, and to take him for a second spell in Division One of the Football League
