## Biography

Warrington born centre half Harold Keenan joined Second Division Blackpool from Atherton in 1912, making his debut for The Seasiders at Fulham in January 1913, making only a single appearance in both that season and the following season, before World War One forced the suspension of peacetime football. However, he resumed with Blackpool in 1919, having actually been stationed in Blackpool, turning out for them regularly during the conflict and becoming a regular choice on the resumption of normal competition and being a regular player in 1920-21 season. He made the last of 109 Blackpool appearances in September 1922.
