## Biography

One of four brothers on Watford's books, three of them professionals. He was versatile and highly respected, latterly as a left full back.

After 16 seasons at the club, six as a first team player, including three as captain, he played for the reserves, it seems, while running The Victory public house in Pinner until his death.

He scored the goal that clinched the southern leaguer title
