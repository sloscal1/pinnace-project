## Biography

Gourlay played in the Home Scots v Anglo-Scots annual trial match in 1914 and was selected for the Scottish Football League XI either side of the war, but never gained a full international cap. His father James Gourlay 1860-1939, played once for Scotland in 1888; they are related to the lawn bowls champions David Gourlay Sr. and David Gourlay Jr Gourley scored the winning goal in the 1922 Cup Final Morton beating Rangers 1-0 at Hampden, the only time Morton have won the trophy.
