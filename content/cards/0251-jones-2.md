## Biography

Jones came from a footballing family. His four brothers – Bryn, Shoni, Emlyn and Bert – were all professional footballers, as were his sons Bryn (Arsenal) and Cliff (Tottenham Hotspur) and nephew Ken.

His great-grandson Scott Neilson is also a footballer.
