## Biography

It seems between 1917 and 1918 Gough made 21appearances for Hibernian in Scotland, these are recorded as a loan, but could simply have been just WW1 guest appearances.

Gough won his only full England international cap on 9 April 1921 against Scotland at Hampden Park, Scotland winning 3–0.
