## Biography

Not only did Tomkins play foe England schoolboys side he was in fact in the very first team that played under that title and also captained the team, Rushden, Northamptonshire born left half Eric Tomkins, joined hometown club Rushden Fosse in 1910 moving to Southern League club Northampton Town a year later.

He played for Northampton both sides of the First World War, guesting for both Southampton and Tottenham Hotspur, during the conflict rejoining the The Cobblers in 1920, playing in their inaugural League fixture at Grimsby Town in August 1920. After 79 appearances during their first three League campaigns, Tomkins joined non league Rushden Town in 1924 but rejoined Northampton Town after a season away, assisting the reserves and making the odd first team appearance, making a further 8 appearances over the next two seasons before returning to Rushden Town in 1927.

As a cricketer he appeared in thirteen first class matches for Northamptonshire from 1920 to 1921 as a right-handed batsman, and scored 204 runs at an average of 10.20 with a highest score of 50 not out.

On retirement he became coach to Midland schools and later Notts County.
