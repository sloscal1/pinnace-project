## Biography

NB: In 1919 Leeds City were expelled from the Football league for allegedly paying players during WW1. The squad were subsequently auctioned off at an hotel in Harrogate, Yorkshire. Affleck was purchased at the auction for £500 (£20,000+ now) along with a second player, Arthur Wainwright for £200 (£8,000+ now)
