## Biography

Scotland : August 10th 1920, v England, 5-4 defeat at Hillsborough, Sheffield, in the British Home Championship.

During WW1 he served with the 51st Div of the Black Watch and sustained several wounds.

After Football Paterson worked as a labourer in the Dundee Works Department.
