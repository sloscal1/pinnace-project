## Biography

Described as 'Cool and discerning between the posts' by Lancashire Post Annual in 1922, Elliott was unable to hold onto his position at any club mainly due to the inclusion of many additional goalkeepers. Elliott was deaf from an injury sustained whilst playing junior football but this did not inhibit his abilities as a keeper.
