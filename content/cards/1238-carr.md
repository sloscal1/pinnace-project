## Biography

A safe impressive goalkeeper, Wednesday spending £1,000 (£49,000+ now) to secure his talents but unusually never played a game for them.

In later life he was the owner of an haulage company in Seaton Delaval, also becoming a qualified pilot and played cricket Co Durham.
