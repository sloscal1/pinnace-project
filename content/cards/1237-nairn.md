## Biography

Scottish born right half Billy Nairn was described as 'the daddy of them all' and club captain at Newport, a superb right half, resolute tackler, and accurate passer of the ball, he was admired and revered , particularly by the younger players.

He joined hometown professional club Cowdenbeath in 1918 before moving to Dundee a year later. He first moved to Wales with Southern League Ebbw Vale in 1920, before joining Third Division (South) Newport County in the summer of 1922, making his Football League debut that August against Southend United. Nairn served Newport County for nine years as a right half or centre half and became club captain, playing representative football for The Welsh League against The Irish Free State League in 1928 before retiring in 1931 after scoring 14 times in 237 appearances for The Ironsides.

He played for Welsh league versus the Irish Free State League in 1928.
