## Biography

Coventry C 1927 16 apps goals

England 1 app 1 goal Home Internation fixture v Ireland October 1921 at Windsor Park Belfast.

Kirton was an avid member of the Temporance Society and as such started his career with Pandon Temperance a Newcastle & District League team before being Spotted by Leeds City joining them in 1919.

Due to financial problems Leeds City folded in 1920 (later becoming Leeds Utd) their players being auctioned off. Villa picked up Kirton for a reported £500.

His 16 appearances as a Coventry City player are not well documented but it is known he transferred to them for a reported fee of £1700 in 1927.

He experienced much success as a Villa player Winning a FA Cup final medal in 1920 against Huddersfield, scoring the only goal of the game in extra time at Stamford Bridge London.

In 1924 he won a runners up medal, against Newcastle Utd in a 2-0 defeat this time at Wembley Stadium, London.

Throughout his life Kirton was a staunch none smoker and drinker, hence his affiliation to the Temeporance Society but, ironically on retirement he purchased and ran a tobacconists/newspaper shop in Sutton Coldfield, he died in a Sutton Coldfield care home in 1970, aged 73yrs.
