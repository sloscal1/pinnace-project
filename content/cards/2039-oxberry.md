## Biography

On the small side for a Centre Forward Oxberry compensated for his lack of physicality with speed and nippines . He had outstanding ball control and sound judgement in his distribution.

He won the Football League Division 2 title with Blackpool in 1930.

Later after a playing career he teamed up with Ted Drake (1912-1995) as his assistant at Chelsea taking them to the Football League Championship in 1954/55 season.
