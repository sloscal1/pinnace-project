## Biography

Local folklore has it that Rovers' goalkeeper Joe Shortt had to be bailed out of police custody on the morning of the Final and that his subsequent performance at Hampden had been affected by the lingering effects of his alcohol consumption the night before.

In 1925/26, whilst at Stenhousemuir, Joe Shortt was offered a £50 bribe to throw a match against Broxburn United, but he rejected the offer and the Stenhousemuir won the match 6–2. A bookmaker from the Bainsford district of Falkirk, was subsequently jailed for attempted match fixing.
