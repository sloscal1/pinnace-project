## Biography

Leonard Graham was an English footballer and first-class cricketer.

Graham made over 300 appearances in the Football League for Millwall. He won two caps for England at full international level, the first, 28th February 1925,Vetch Field, Swansea v Wales, a 2-1 win, in the British Home Championship and the second in the same competition at Hampden Park, Glasgow, v Scotland a 2-0 defeat.

He also represented the Football League XI.

Graham was a right-handed batsman who played first-class cricket for Essex. A lower-order batsman, Graham made two appearances for Essex during the 1926 season, scoring a total of 14 runs.

In 1935, he replaced Syd Puddefoot (1894-1972) (see page 322 323 covering Falkirk), as an FA instructor to Kent Secondary Schoolboys.
