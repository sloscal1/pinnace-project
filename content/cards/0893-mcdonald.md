## Biography

McDonald was born in Inverness, Scotland and played for Rangers in his early career; the Glasgow club won the Scottish Football League in both the seasons he was with them, but he only had a minor role making only 2 appearances.

He joined Newcastle United in 1921, for a fee of £2,000 (£80,000+ now), making his debut on the 5th March 1921 against Middlesbrough, then spending a decade with the club.

At 5' 8" he was one of Newcastle's taller forwards of the time, described as an unselfish player he formed a formidable partnership with Stan Seymour on the wing.

McDonald served in the Royal Horse Artillery during the First World War

He joined York City in May 1931.

After football he became a Time Keeper at Vickers and also looked after the St James Park press box, he followed the Magpies, as a fan, until his death.
