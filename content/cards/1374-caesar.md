## Biography

According to ENFA, between 1923 and 1934 Caesar had associations, as an amateur, with all of the following teams. Dulwich x 3 1923 1925 & 1928 , Darlington, Fulham, London Caledonians, Chelsea, Walsall, Brentford, Hayes, Wimbledon, Egham, Leyton, Civil Service, Kingstonian, and Harwich & Parkstone, other than 8 appearances at Walsall in 1927-28 and one at Fulham in1925-25 and Brentford 1929-30, there are no further stats existing on his playing career.

According to insertions on Wikipedia Caesar was also a Cricketer, playing for Surrey in 1922 and Somerset in 1946.
