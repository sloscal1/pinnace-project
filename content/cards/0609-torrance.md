## Biography

Joining Fulham from Glasgow side, Ashfield in 1910, he spent 16 years at the club, making a total of 357 league and cup appearances, scoring 40 goals. A utility forward, he played in four different attacking positions for the "Cottagers", throughout his time at Craven Cottage.

He moved to Walsall in July 1926, playing in forty league and cup games for "Saddlers", but the club only avoided applying for re-election by two points, and he left Fellows Park at the end of the season.

Torrance worked as a ships' boilermaker in Scotland during the First World War. He later worked for a telephone company and died of cancer in July 1949.
