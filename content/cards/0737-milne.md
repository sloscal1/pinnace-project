## Biography

A rugged no nonsense tackler, possessing a strong kick, he was recruited by Stoke at the age of 23, as part of a rebuild capable of winning the Southern league to regain league status.

He commenced as a left half back but after a poor start in that position he was moved to right full back where he found his value. Moving across to the left to accommodate George Turner on the right, Milne was the recipient of a Southern League Championship medal in 1915 when he was an ever present.

He didn't play much during WW1 having been called back to the North East to work in the munitions industry, however following the end of the conflict he returned to playing and retired at the age of 41.
