## Biography

Thomas Gable Smith was an English footballer who played as an inside right or outside right in the Football League for South Shields, Leicester City, Manchester United, Northampton Town and Norwich City.

Smith, known as 'Tosher', played for Marsden Villa and Whitburn before moving to South Shields in 1919. He went on to spend four seasons at Manchester United and three at Northampton Town.

Four of his six brothers were also footballers. William Henry 1906-1983 and John William 1898-1977 both played for South Shields and played in 1934 FA Cup Final together for Portsmouth. Septimus Charles 1912-2006 later played for Leicester City, and Joseph Kirby 1908-1993 played reserve football for Leicester and later played for Watford.
