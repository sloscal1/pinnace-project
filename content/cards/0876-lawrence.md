## Biography

NB: predominantly an understudy to Ernie Scattergood during his first 3 seasons with Derby, establishing himself as first choice in 1914, playing 31 League games as Derby won the Second Division Championship in 1914-15. His career was interrupted by World War One, but on resumption he spent 3 seasons as Derby's primary glovesman, before losing his place to Ben Olney in 1922.
