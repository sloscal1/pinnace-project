## Biography

McIntyre cost Derby a £10 signing on fee (£380 today) from Stenhousemuir and never was a tenner spent more wiser as he gave Derby 10 plus years good service. Starting out as an inside forward it was his conversion to right, wing half where he found his vocation. Described as a player of polish, precision and determination he retired in 1933, he later served as coach, assistant manager and scout to the club.
