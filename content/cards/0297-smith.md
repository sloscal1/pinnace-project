## Biography

A Wikipedia entry, sights Smith as one of Palace's greatest striker quoting 124 goals in 192 appearances, although Crystal Palace a complete Record by Mike Purkiss, Breedon Book series, only mentions 25 league appearances and 11 goals despite referencing he played at Palace whilst in the southern league, pre 1920's.
