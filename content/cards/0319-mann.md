## Biography

NB: The second Anomalie. Again another player from this card collection with little or nothing written about him, Football league players records 1888 to 1939 by Michael Joyce, reference a player with the above details, inferring playing at Newport at the relevant period the card collection was assembled. Whilst the playing record is not definitive, there is reference to time at West Bromwich Albion, Walsall and Newport County. Also, in the picture he wears a striped shirt similar to those worn by West Brom players in the 1920's. It would be fair to say that Jack Mann and the picture, are/could be, one and the same.
