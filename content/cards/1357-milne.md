## Biography

Milne was the son of the first Aberdeen Chairman, Baillie Milne, and by the time he reached Aston Villa he was already a qualified doctor of Medicine.

Showing great prowess as a footballer in the early 1900's for Aberdeen Boys brigade and representing City Boys he played for Strath in 1914 and Aberdeen University in 1915 signing for Aberdeen as an amateur in or around 1920.

Milne transferred to Aston Villa in 1923 and continued to be a regular member of their first team subsequently playing in Villa's 1924 FA Cup final team defeated, 2-0 by Newcastle United at Wembley in 1924 where Milne was, unusually, the only Scot apart from team manager George Ramsay, in Villa's team whilst Newcastle fielded five.

He played his last game for Villa in April 1929 in an away fixture at Leicester City retiring in May 1929 where later he became club doctor at Villa from 1930 to 1933.
