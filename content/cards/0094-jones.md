## Biography

England 1 appearance, 10h may 1923 v France, in Paris, a friendly winning 4-1. 1924 Football league XI v Ireland, in Belfast.

In February 1914 Jones experienced a broken leg, which threatened his career in Football, but, by October of the same year, he returned to take his place in the team. Described by the press of the day as "a player with courage and dash"
