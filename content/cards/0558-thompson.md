## Biography

Signed by Bristol City for £500 (£17,000+ today). He stayed there for two years before joining Bath City.

Following his retirement from playing he became a cinema manager in Treharris, South Wales until 1939 after which he worked for J. S. Fry & Sons, the chocolate manufacturer where he trained the company football team. He died in Bath in 1969 following a car accident.
