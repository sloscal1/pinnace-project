## Biography

Sargeaunt worked as a slater mechanic in Newcastle On Tyne. His father, Henry Edward George Sargeaunt, was a musician born in Toronto who served in the military. His father was killed in 1915 while serving with the Durham Light Infantry. Later in life he worked as milling machine mechanic.
