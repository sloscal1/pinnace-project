## Biography

Buchan, was the older brother of the more famous, Charles 'Charlie' Buchan 1891-1960. His early career was spent with several clubs including, Woodhall Thistle, Sunderland (1910), Leyton and Sunderland Rovers. He finished his career at Runcorn and Atherton Collieries, in the Lancashire lower leagues.
