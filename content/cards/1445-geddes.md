## Biography

Geddes commenced his footballing life, which was probably impaired by the First World War, starting with several Lanarkshire junior sides before joining St Mirren in 1920, research infers he only made 3 appearances for the club before moving to Albion Rovers for 5 years.

In 1926 he ventured south signing for Division three south side Bristol City but although he was present during that season where City won promotion from that Division he only made 8 appearances before returning north of the border to Morton.
