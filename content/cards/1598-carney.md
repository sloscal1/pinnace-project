## Biography

Carney's career was finished after sustaining a Cartledge injury at the age of 36. He settled in Newport working as joiner in a steel tube works. In the 1950's he managed Newport's 'A' team.

It is thought that he had early career associations with both Bolton Wanderers and Blackpool, but if that is true, neither team actually registered him as a player.
