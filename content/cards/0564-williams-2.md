## Biography

Williams, always known by his middle name of Rees, joined The Wednesday in 1922 for £1500 ( £71,000+ now) giving several good seasons to the club, his best season being 1925/26 season as Wednesday won the Division two title.

The arrival of Mark Hooper (1901-1974) reduced his appearance and he was transferred to Manchester United in 1928 for £2000 ( £95.000+ now).

He made 8 appearances for Wales, four, whilst at Wednesday, the others at Manchester United, scoring two goals between 1921 and 1929. His two goals were scored in a 2-2 draw with Ireland.
