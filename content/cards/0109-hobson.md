## Biography

A solid reliable defender, Bert Hobson, secured a regular place at Roker Park, from October 1913 and retained it in the post war years until his departure. He was offered a maximum terms to further his contract in May 1922, but refused these unless granted a benefit match. Its thought his time at Rochdale and dalliances at West Stanley and Jarrow were probably loan periods.

Sunderland, put him up for transfer asking £1,500 ( £71,000+ now), for his services, later reduced to £750 ( £35,000+ now).

Bert returned to league football but with little success with Darlington, its not known if a fee was actually involved. He subsequently returned to local junior football with Jarrow and Spennymoor United, before retiring all together.
