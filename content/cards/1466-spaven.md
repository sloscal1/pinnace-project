## Biography

Courageous and powerfully built, undisputed favorite of the forest spectators, could bustle past defenders, possessing a powerful and accurate shot, his only weakness being, was his head work.

Honors : Nottingham Forest : Division Two Championship 1921/22 & Top Scorer with 18 goals.
