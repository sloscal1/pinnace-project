## Biography

Hughes was captain of the England Schoolboys team then played locally for Castletown before signing as an amateur for Sunderland in July 1919. Joe Palmer signed Hughes in September 1920 for Bristol City.

Sharing the full back positions with Ernie Glenn (1902-1965). He won the third Division (North) title in 1926-27 season.

After retirement Hughes was landlord of the "Old Inn" at Hutton near Weston-super-Mare.
