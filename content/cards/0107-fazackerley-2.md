## Biography

Described as a clever skilfull player with a powerful shot. After early stints at home town club, Preston North End and then Accrington plus a period in the American Soccer League, he moved to Hull City for £50 (£4,000+ now), later moving to Sheffield United for £1000 (£95,000+ now), at that time, a club record fee. He became a main stay of the team and played a large part in the club's success in the FA Cup in 1914/15, scoring the second United goal by heading in a shot from Wally Masterman (1888-1965) that had rebounded from the bar ensuring that he picked up a winners medal.

He eventually joined Everton for £4,000 (£149,000+ now) - a huge amount for the time - although Stan's own part in the transfer led to an FA enquiry. Further honours came with Wolves winning the 1923/24 season Third Division North league.
