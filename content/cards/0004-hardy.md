## Biography

England 1907 to 1920 21 apps 0 goals. All whilst with Aston Villa or Liverpool

First Cap Feb 16th 1907 Ireland 1-0 win at Goodison Park, Liverpool.

Final cap April 10th 1920 v Scotland 5-4 win Hillsborough Sheffield.

Hardy is some what a footballing legend, he was a relative of Stan Hardy Nottingham Forest manager 1930/31 season.

Hardy enlisted in the British Army during the first world war, whilst he lived in Jesmond, Northumberland, England.

He served the Roal Northumberland Fusiliers & the Machine gun corp achieving the rank of lieutenant.

He was discharged before the war concluded having been gassed whilst serving on the Western Front.

There is reference stating he was also an ordinary seaman with the Royal Navy during that conflict.
