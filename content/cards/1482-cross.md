## Biography

Benjamin Cross was an English professional footballer who played as an inside forward for Burnley.

Cross was born in Birkenhead and played for junior teams there. During the first World War he played for Liverpool, and then joined Burnley which at that time were one of the best UK teams. He Forged a famous attacking partnership with Walter Weaver (card 1481 above), and captained England schoolboys.

Holds an English league championship medal for 1920–21 season.
