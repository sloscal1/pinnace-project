## Biography

Loughran played for Workington, Usworth Colliery, Sunderland West End, Carlisle Utd, Spennymoor Utd, Usworth Colliery, St Josephs CYMS (Sunderland) between 1923 & 1934 NB: It seems as with many in this Ashington group of players, most were probably part time players plying their footballing skill at varying clubs around the North West and East. This is evident with Loughran re joining the Usworth Colliery team several times.
