## Biography

Smart was born in Blackheath, which was then in Staffordshire. He had four brothers and four sisters. He played schools football in the Rowley Regis area before joining Blackheath Town, and when his career was interrupted by the outbreak of the WW1, he played Army football. After the war, he played for Halesowen Town before signing for Football League First Division club Aston Villa in January 1920 for a £300.00 fee (£11,000+ now).

He soon established himself in Villa's first team, and made his debut for the England team against Scotland in 1921. The third of his five appearances, also against Scotland, was in the first match that England played at Wembley Stadium, on 12 April 1924.
