## Biography

Described as fast and tricky, primarily a left winger, who was comfortable playing on either wing Harrold Started his career in the Birmingham & District leagues, later playing for Leicester during the last year of WW1 and formally joining the club on resumption of post war football, He made 19 league and cup appearances, scoring one goal, before joining Nottingham Forest in 1920.

A regular member of the Forest team at the City Ground, he was a member of the team that won promotion from the second division in 1922.

Harrold lost his place to the arrival of Sid Gibson (see following page) and moved to Accrington Stanley in 1922 on the recommendation of Forest Centre Forward Pat Nelis (1898-1970) (see following page), who had played for Accrington.

Whilst at Stanley he suffered a serious break to his leg and twisted ankle, which subsequently curtailed his football career retiring during 1922-23 season. However, his injury did not stop him turning to his other sporting love, Cricket.

He was granted a testimonial match in May 1922, with combined Lancashire & Yorkshire team playing a combined Nottingham Forest & Leicester team, 3,000 people attending.
