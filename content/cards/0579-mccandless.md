## Biography

Ireland 5 apps, first 13th April 1912 v Wales Home Championship a 3-2 defeat at Ninian Park, Cardiff, Wales. His last v England 23rd October 1920 Home Championship a 2-0 defeat Roker Park, Sunderland, England.

McCandless spent much of his career at Bradford PA but was loaned to Hibernian Scotland in1917 and Linfield 1918 but these could have been wartime guest appearances. Between 1923 and 1926 he spent time at several clubs, Barn (Ireland) Mid Rhondda Utd (Wales) Lovells Athletic (Wales) and Coleraine as player Manager in 1926.
