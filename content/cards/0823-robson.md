At the commencement of season 1920/21 South Shields recruited 46 players, including George Robson and his brother Joseph William Robson (1899-1961) (not included in the Pinnace Collection cards), included, amongst the 46 recruits, were nine full backs.

With, Warney Cresswell (card 76) and Alf Maitland (card 825) being the regular pairing, it was sometime before, short and stocky, George Robson, broke into the first team, his best season being 29 appearances in 1924/25 season.

Henceforth, Robsons career seemed to meander from team to team, a short period at Southampton where only ever turning out for the reserves was followed by his only other consistent period of playing, at Ashington where he made 21 appearances between 1927 and 1929.

His playing record is vague, but, it is known, he won the Scottish Victory Cup playing for St Mirren in 1919/20.
