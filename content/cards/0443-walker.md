## Biography

Walker served in the British Army during the First World War and the malaria he contracted in Palestine eventually ended his football career.

NB: He was the first Fulham player to receive a red card in a Football League match, versus Bradford City on 15 February 1913.

I cannot validate this piece of information, as red cards did not come into use until recently, but, I'm sure the author's meaning is, that he was the first player to be dismissed in a league game for Fulham
