## Biography

Castle later took up several coaching positions in Holland, namely, Ajax1927-28, ZAC 1928-30, PEC 1930, Heerenveen 1932, Meppeler Sports Club, 1933-35, later returning to Heerenveen, between 1936 and 1938. His reasons for taking up these posts is not clear but it would infer his period working in Holland may have been brought to a close by the advent of WW2.
