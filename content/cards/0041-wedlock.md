## Biography

England 26 apps 2 goals first v Ireland 16th February 1907 Home Championship 1-0 win at Goodison Park Liverpool. Last v Wales 16th March 1914 Home Championship 2-0 win at Ninian Park, Cardiff, Wales.

Wedlock also known as "Fatty" or the "India Rubber Man", was a footballer who played for Bristol City from 1905 until his retirement in 1921. Between 1901 and 1905 he played for Aberdare. He was a centre-half whose his short and stout stature belied his natural talent. He won 26 England caps between 1907 and 1914, his only rival for the centre-half position being Charlie Roberts 1883-1939 of Manchester United, his opposite number in the 1909 FA Cup Final.

The East End at Ashton Gate Stadium was named the Wedlock Stand in his honour, before being demolished in 2014 as part of the Ashton Gate Stadium redevelopment. Wedlock's pub (now demolished) opposite the ground, and watering hole for fans up until the redevelopment, was where he lived and worked for 43 years. Folk singer Fred Wedlock 1942-2010, was Billy's grandson.
