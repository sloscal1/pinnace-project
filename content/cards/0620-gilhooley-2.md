## Biography

Scotland : v Wales, Wrexham, Wales, 4th February 1922, 2-1 defeat in the British Home Championship Born near Edinburgh to Irish parents, the family moved to Fife in Scotland to follow the Shale mining industry, growing up in impoverished surroundings.

His early football was played with local junior side Glencraig Celtic catching the eye of Celtic manager Willie Maley (1868-1958) who signed him, following a trial, in 1913. Eventually he was seen as being too young and raw for Celtic and was farmed out to Abercorn and Vale of Leven.

During WW1 he served with the 17th Highland Infantry, for the full duration, seeing action at the front line, being promoted to the rank of corporal.

He survived the conflict losing a couple of fingers and thumb.

After demob he returned to football with Clydebank for two seasons, but from there on and despite a couple of moves for substantial fees, injuries blighted the remainder of his career, briefly retiring during 1924/25 season before returning to the game with Bradford City, only to find he would always be dogged with re-occurring injuries.

Following football he was employed as an Insurance inspector in the Glasgow area.
