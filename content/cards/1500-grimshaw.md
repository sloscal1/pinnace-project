## Biography

Commenced a career in football with Livingstone United in Scotland, he moved down to play in Lancashire with Barnoldswick United in 1909 before brief dalliances with Burnley and Colne, prior to signing for Bradford City.

After retirement he became a licensee In Bradford Yorkshire.
