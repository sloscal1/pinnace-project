## Biography

Information on players in the Accrington Stanley section of the Pinnace Collection are very vague and Walker posed quite difficult player to pinpoint on the internet, and within statistical book information.

Eventually I came up with this information using a vague entry in Wikipedia and a statistical book, Football Players League Records 1888 to 1939 compiled by Michael Joyce.

Whilst the card shows Walker wearing what looks like a goalkeeper's roll neck, he was in fact a Centre Forward converted to Half Back, I feel the top shown in the card image is possibly a training top or in fact he did stand in as a keeper from time to time.

Its thought that Walker hailing from a mining family, played much of his early football in Scottish junior league's.

In 1910 invited for a trial by Dundee he later moved from his home in Cowdenbeath to Dundee, signing for the club. His appearances were limited, but in 1912/13 season he made 20 appearances scoring 7 goals.

His form as striker and apparently a penalty taker, alerted many English league clubs and in 1913 he signed for Chelsea, making an impressive 2 goal debut v Oldham Athletic.

Having dropped to playing half back, again, his appearances became limited but he did appear in Chelsea's FA cup Final defeat to Sheffield Utd at Old Trafford Manchester in 1915, known as the Kharki Cup Final due to the amount of servicemen attending the game.

Post World War one he played three more times before leaving for Newport County in 1920.

Then followed his spell with Accrington in 1922.

In 1923 he left the UK, alone, to travel to the USA, to play for Fall River. Later he would marry his wife in Fulham London before bringing her to America where they emigrated completely. There are no playing records of his time in America.

Walker died in Los Angeles, California, in 1964.
