## Biography

Considered by many, including Blackburn, not to have the physique of a Centre Forward, Joe had natural body swerve and strong shot in either foot bagging him an average of 20 goals a season, leading the scoring charts at Nelson for 5 consecutive seasons before moving onto Swindon for £250 (£12,000+ now) where he continued on the goal trail.
