## Biography

Walker moved to Nottingham Forest for a fee of £2,500 (£125,000+ now) in May 1923.

A strong bustling forward blessed with pace and the ability to take passes on the run either in the air or along the ground.

Following serious injury he returned to Scots football and later became president of Bo'ness FC
