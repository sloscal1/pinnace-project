## Biography

Harold found stiff competition for his left-wing position from Albert Pearson (Card 292 see page 479) and was restricted to just nine league appearances in his debut season. Described as having "splendid turn of speed" he was critised, having, "a desire to delay crossing the ball which allows a defence to take up position again."

After 4 seasons at the club he moved onto, Leicester City.

Being releaed by Millwall from, 1932 to 1934, he played for Oswestry Town, Canterbury Waverley, and Cray Wanderers NB: Walter Wadsworth 1890-1949, a Centre Half, was at the club at the time the Pinnace Collection was being prepared but is not included included, in the card set, for some reason.
