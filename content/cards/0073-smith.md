## Biography

A member of a famous footballing family of footballers, Jack Smith had three brothers all well known players.

William Henry Smith (1906-1983) and Thomas Gable Smith (1900-1934) both played for South Shields, Thomas servicing other well known clubs including Manchester City, Northampton and Norwich City, perhaps the best known brother is Septimus Charles 'Sep' Smith (1912-2006), who played for Leicester City.

Described as an exceptionally talented Inside Forward.

When South Shields manager at the time, Jack Tinn (1878-1971), left to manage Portsmouth, he had no hesitation in signing Smith for Pompy, where he represented England during 1931, scoring 4 goals in three appearances.

At Pompy he was a member the beaten FA cup final team that lost to Bolton Wanderers in 1929 and Manchester City in 1934..

England activity : October 17th, 1931 v Ireland, 6-2 win in Belfast, v Wales, at Anfield, Liverpool, November 18th, 1931, 3-1 win Smith scoring in both games, British Home Championship.

Last appearance December 9th 1931, at Highbury, London, friendly v Spain a 7-1 win, Smith scoring twice.
