## Biography

Aston Villa : FA Cup semi-finalist 1914. He was almost certainly the younger brother of Frank Edgley (1880-1910) (with whom he shares a physical likeness), also born in Crewe and a Crewe Alexandra graduate, who played for Sheffield United, Fulham, Reading and Hartlepools United.
