## Biography

Len was part of the Hawtin dynasty that had, for years, connections with the Cobblers, both as player and club directors.

An amateur all of his career.

Despite only being used by the club as cover, he was selected to play for an FA representative side to play Oxford University, unfortunately dates and details of this match are not available.

Thanks to 'Northampton Town Who's Who', by Frank Grande, for this piece and minor details throughout the Northampton section of the book.
