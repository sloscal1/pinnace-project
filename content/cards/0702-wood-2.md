## Biography

There is little known about Wood but it appears he retired after leaving Blackpool and given his playing record details, he, like many of his time, following WW1, travelled and played for several North American Soccer league teams, where money was in abundance. It could be that he actually stayed and fully emigrated to the U.S. but this is not documented anywhere.
