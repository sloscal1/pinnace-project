## Biography

A former Schoolboy International making two appearances at that level in 1915. After a brief stay at South Shields he somewhat lost his way, in football, travelling the country in search of a club, having his best period whilst at Tranmere Rovers.

He later dropped into none league football with Workington where he did score over 130 goals for the Cumbrian team, assisting them to reach round four of the FA cup in 1933/34 season.
