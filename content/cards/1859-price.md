## Biography

It is said Price greatly improved and strengthened the Nelson defence, when he arrived at the centre of defence from Scotland.

Described as a tireless worker, whose heading was a feature of his game, tackling well, and having effective passing skills.

Leaving Nelson he served Ashington well, and on retirement remained at the club, as trainer.
