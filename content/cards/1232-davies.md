## Biography

Corinthians

Nelson, Lancashire born left winger Arthur Davies was an amateur footballer who played for The Corinthians after the First World War and who won an England Amateur international cap when he played in a 2-0 defeat to Wales at Molineux in January 1921. He joined Third Division (South) Northampton Town that summer and made his Football League debut against Brentford that October, scoring his only goal for The Cobblers in a 1-0 victory over Watford the following month. He made 12 appearances for Northampton Town during the 1921-22 season before returning to play for The Corinthians.

Davies was an amateur International footballer while still at University. He applied to play for Northampton Town when he took a teaching post in the town. A fleet footed footballer, but by the nature of his work, he was not always available for away games.

NB in the photograph he appears to be wearing a Corinthians shirt, although the image was published during his spell with Northampton Town, and is not duplicated with the Corinthian section of the collection.
