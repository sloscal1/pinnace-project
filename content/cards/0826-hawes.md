## Biography

Son of a long serving Norwich City assistant trainer, Hawes had a thorough grounding in the area of junior football prior to joining the club.

Once at Norwich City a reputation as a goal scoer was enhanced, scoring 79 times for the club in WW1 friendlies, including seven hat-tricks.

Catching the eye of South Shields, he signed for them in May 1920 and enhanced his reputation there over the next seasons and a half, scoring 19 goals in 54 league and cup matches, Sunderland paid a fee of £1700 in December 1921.

His arrival at Roker Park was hereladed by a debut brace, in a 5-0 win over West Bromwich Albion, scoring 39 goals in 147 league and cup appearances, over the next several seasons.

His form herealded a trial for the rest versus England in February 1925, but caps did not follow.

In August 1927 he moved on, Bradford Park Avenue, paying £1750 for his services.

He scored 18 goals in 54 league and cup appearances, winning a Third Division (North) championship medal in 1928.

A season with Nelson, drawing a fee of £100 in May 1931, ended prematurely when he was injured.

At the grand age of 37 he moved onto Rochdale, his final league club, for a fee of £50 in May 1931.
