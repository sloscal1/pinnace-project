## Biography

Arthur Mercer had great aspirations to be a jockey and was with Arthur Wootton's racing stables for time but grew too big for the role.

Arthurs elder brother David Mercer (1893-1950) who was an FA cup winner with Sheffield United in 1925, and later, twice played for England.

As he aspired in football he commanded several strong fees, being transferred to Bury, in 1925, for £750 (£38,000+ now), Sheffield United, in 1926 for £700 (£36,000+ now), where he hooked up for a while playing alongside brother David.

Following a short stint in non league football, Bristol City paid £450 (£24,000+ now) for his services in 1930.

Overall it is apparent his career never met it's early aspirations.
