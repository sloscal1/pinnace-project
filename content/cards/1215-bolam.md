## Biography

Back in te day, according to the Telegraph and Star, they wrote, "he is speedy and invariably puts across a good ball, whilst his ability to use both feet enables him to meet his man with confidence and success".

A match preview in the Derby Daily Telegraph suggested his problem was temperamental: he "can play on either wing, and in the mood can be really dangerous, but he allows himself to become irritated, and goes off his game very easily."
