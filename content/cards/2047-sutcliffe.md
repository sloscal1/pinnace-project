## Biography

*Sutcliffe was obviously at Leeds City at the time of their resignation from the league, most players were auctioned off, other clubs paying a fee for the more valuable players, but, it appears that there is no record of this happening in Sutcliffe's case, as he was probably a peripheral player who had not made the first team. He was subsequently picked up by Rotherham County.
