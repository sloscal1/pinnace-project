## Biography

No relation to fellow team mate David Mercer, William was originally an outside left until player shortages at Prescot found him playing goal, a position he continued playing, whilst still being Prescot's penalty taker. Being deposed at Hull City, he transferred to Huddersfield for £2,000 (£98,000+ now) where he subsequently won an hat-trick of league titles, and an FA cup runners up medal in 1928. He was a fine all round sportsmen playing cricket and was a good Billiards player, once beating renown snooker champion Joe Davis.
