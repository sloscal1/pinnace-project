## Biography

Born in Leytonstone, he became the youngest ever professional at 15 years of age, when in 1921 he signed for West Ham United from Fairbairn House. On 6 May 1922 (making an appearance against Blackpool) at aged 16 years and 221 days, he became West Ham's youngest ever player; a record he held until Reece Oxford made his debut 23 days younger in 2015.

Following his season at Chelsea, he became a free agent going on to serve amateur teams, Dagenham, Guildford and Dartford playing until the age of 40.

Leaving football he was involved in the haulage business in some way and ran a tobacconists in Ilford, Essex.
