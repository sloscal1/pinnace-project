## Biography

Moved Notts County from Aston Villa due to limited playing time under studying to England International Arthur Dorrell (Card 677 page Page 7). Arriving at Notts County he immediately made an impact in the Division Two Championship winning team of 1923. Described as quick off the mark and an accurate crosser of the ball.
