## Biography

Harper is another player who dipped his foot in the Atlantic and played in the newly formed American Soccer League.

He also Played 11 times for the full Scottish side and twice for the Scottish League XI.

He won titles with Arsenal, Hibernian, Scotland and whilst in the American Soccer League.
