## Biography

It seems that Reid played his early football for Peebles Rovers in Scottish Junior Football Trialing at both Partick Thistle and Chelsea he joined Lincoln City becoming a regular in 1910 playing in excess of 30 games for them.

Moving onto Airdrie in 1912, and playing as a Centre forward for Airdrie, he finished as the top scorer in Scottish Football League Division One in the 1912–13 and 1913–14 seasons; he converted to an outside right after World War I to accommodate the emerging Hughie Gallacher,( see page 23 card 1254) and was on the wing in the team that won the Scottish Cup in 1924; the Diamonds were also runners-up in Division One four consecutive times in that period.

He was club captain for a long period of his 15 year tenure at the club.

Reid was selected three times by the Scottish national team (plus two further unofficial wartime internationals) and also played for the Scottish Football League XI, scoring six times in five appearances (and another in a wartime fundraising match).

He died in hospital at Airdrie in April 1938 at the young age of 47 years.
