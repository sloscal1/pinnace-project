## Biography

Also played at none league, Bromsgrove Rovers & Worcester City

England 1921 to 1924 5 apps 0 goals First app v Ireland 22nd October 1921 1-1 draw Windsor Park Belfast.

Last app v Scotland April 12th 1924 1-1 draw at Wembley Stadium London. Coincidently both Kirton & Walker also Villa players scored in these games and the England team was captained by Moss.

Moss was a young fringe player until injury brought him into the 1920 FA Cup winning team where he played a 'blinder' virtually blotting out Huddersfield Town's twin strikers, Mann & Taylor, World war one intervened in this players fine career where he played briefly for Walsall, as a guest whilst serving with the 4th Lincolnshire Regiment receiving a career threatening knee wound. Recovering well he returned to England becoming an Army PT instructor.

He would go on to be formidable defender for Villa and latterly Cardiff, captaining both Villa and England before joining none league Bromsgrove as player manager.

Moss's two sons all played professional football , Frank (Jnr) 1938-1955, Worcester City, Wolves Sheff Wednesday, & Villa 296 apps and Amos 1937-1956 103 apps for Villa
