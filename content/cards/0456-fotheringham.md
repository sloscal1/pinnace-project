## Biography

Fotheringham started his career with his hometown junior side Larkhill Thistle.

He served the mentioned clubs with aplomb making over 580 league and cup appearances for his 6 clubs.

With QotS he had moderate success helping them to their highest league placing 4th in 1933-34 season and was in the team that kept them in the division the following season reaching the cup quarter finals beating Rangers on the way.

One amusing anecdote about Fotheringham was that in one game v Arbroath he inadvertently left his false teeth behind in the changing rooms the teeth subsequently being returned to him via a Fish lorry driver the following week!.

He was part of a 16 man Queen ot the South squad that toured Algiers in an invitation tour tournament in 1936 returning with a winners medal beating Racing Santander in the final.

He managed, St Mirren in 1942 winning the summer cup v Glasgow Rangers the following season. Leaving the Saints in 1945 due to illness he returned to manage Cowdenbeath five years before taking over at Airdrie but was sacked following relegation.

He later scouted for Queen ot the South.
