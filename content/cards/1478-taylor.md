## Biography

Moving from Rangers to Bradford City in September 1910, Taylor signed for Burnley in November 1911. With Bradford he won the 1911 FA Cup Final, and with Burnley he won the 1914 FA Cup Final and the 1920–21 Football League title.

A pacy player but at the end of 1910-11 season, he suffered from serious heart trouble which put his career and health at risk, He recovered to continue with Burnley winning said titles, and playing in two Home Scots v Anglo-Scots international trials just prior to the war, but never gained a full cap for Scotland.

He later managed St Johnstone and Dunfermline Athletic, before returning to England to manage Carlisle United.
