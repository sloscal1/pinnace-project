## Biography

Malcolm McPhail played as an outside left, primarily for Kilmarnock, with whom he won the Scottish Cup in 1920. He was selected to play for Scotland in an unofficial 'Victory International' in 1919. He also represented a Scottish League XI in 1917.

McPhail later served as a director of St Mirren. He was the older brother of Rangers and Scotland forward Robert Lowe 'Bob' McPhail, (1905-2000).
