## Biography

Hampson was the brother of footballers Billy Hampson 1882-1966 and Tommy Hampson 1898-1929. He served as a gunner in the Royal Garrison Artillery during the First World War. In January 1917, Hampson was treated for synovitis (arthritic Condition) of the right knee. He was discharged from the Army in September 1917
