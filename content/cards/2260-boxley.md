## Biography

Boxley was the youngest of three footballing brothers. Fred Boxley 1886-1955 was Wolves' principal goalkeeper between 1910 and 1912, and David Boxley 1890-1941 played for Stoke including in League football in the season immediately following the First World War. One of the brothers' sons, John 'Jack' Boxley 1931-2016, also born in Cradley Heath, played with Bristol City and Coventry City during the 1950's.
