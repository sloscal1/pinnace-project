## Biography

Speaking with other historians about this player there is disputes over his birth details some sources show this person as George Henry Dale 2nd May 1883, dying in 1957 which would mean his career started at the age of 31. In support of the Belverdere birth place a report in the Daily News reported on the 27th of September 1930 that a George Dale, formerly of Notts County, QPR, and Chelsea had been appointed as coach to Erith & Belverdere FC.

Either way it seems Dale probably combined a professional football career, juggled with ordinary domestic routine working.
