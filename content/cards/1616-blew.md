## Biography

After his retirement, Blew played a role in scouting young players for Wrexham, including Arfon Griffiths MBE, who later played for Arsenal and made more than 550 appearances for Wrexham.

His father was Horace Blew (1878-1957), also a footballer who played at full-back for Wrexham, Manchester United, Manchester City and the Welsh national team.

Frank also represented Wales at amateur level
