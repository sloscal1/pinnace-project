## Biography

Taylors career in football was effected, like many with the intrusion of WW1.

Stationed in Ripon Yorkshire, the posting led to him signing professionally for Burnley, there his opportunities were limited by the pescence of club captain Thomas William 'Tommy' Boyle (1886-1940). Oldham paid £2,000 (£73,000+ now) for him in 1920.There he blossomed being an automatic selection for 5 years, appearing in all three half back positions. He finished his career back in Nottinghamshire playing for market town side, Newark Town.
