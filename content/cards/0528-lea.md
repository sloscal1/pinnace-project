## Biography

Little is known of Lea, but consulting the internet I found a story run in the Oswestry Gazette in May 2015, which concerned the shirt Lea wore during the 1921 FA cup final at Stamford Bridge London, a 1-0 defeat at the hands of Tottenham Hotspur

The match was played in heavy rain and at half time the Wolves players changed to dry shirts which bore the club crest and club logo 'Out of the darkness cometh light' . Allegedly all of the original shirts disappeared during the second half but its thought Lea never changed his. In May 2015 the shirt went up for auction and was bought for £6500 and is displayed in Wolverhampton Museum. It is also thought that the name 'Tancy' was a Moniker which was given to members of the Navy during WW1 who were called either Lea or Lee. (See following page 144)
