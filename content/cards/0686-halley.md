## Biography

Played once, in 1910, for a Scottish representative XI. He Was also selected for Scots v English XI's trials on three occasions, 1913,1914 & 1922 without being awarded a cap.

Halley served in the British Army prior to the First World War. He served as a sapper in the Royal Engineers during the war and saw action in France and Mesopotamia (middle east), before being posted to India in 1919. After his retirement from football, Halley worked as a plasterer and studied at Ruskin College, Oxford. He died at Victoria Hospital, Burnley on 18 December 1941.
