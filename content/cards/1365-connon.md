## Biography

Connon playing junior football for both Carlton & Richmond signed for home town club Aberdeen in 1919, having played as a guest for them during WW1 where he served with the Royal Artillery. He five goals in Seven scored wartime appearances.

As his career ended he took up a position working as an Insurance agency superintendent for the Royal Insurance Company .

His son Bill Connon 1922-2000 was a Rugby Union player for Aberdeen from 1940 to 1950 becoming President of the Scottish Rugby Union.
