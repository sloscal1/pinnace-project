## Biography

Reed's performances were typical of players who came from the north east, he was tough tackling, solid, determined and efficient.

Arriving at the club he understudied other players and spent some 10 years at the club before breaking into the first team, this period interjected by WW1.

During the war he was a Sergeant Instructor in the 2nd battalion of the Royal Fusiliers, spending time, serving in Surrey, Northumberland and Edinburgh.

As a reserve player he won the Birmingham District league in 1920 and captained the reserves to the Central League title in 1922/23 season.

He later became trainer at the club overseeing many league and cup successes during the 1930's and was sponge man at two England versus the rest games.

He was also masseur at Warwickshire CCC.
