## Biography

'Jack' of all trades, it seems, Armstrong could play anywhere in the team, and always play well, regularly filling in at all positions including attack. Slightly built, he made up for this lack of size, with speed and skill, being particularly clever in the tackle, then distributing the ball well. Following the war he became captain and accrued a, then, club record appearance tally, of 459 league and cup appearances before moving on in 1923 to Sutton Town as a player coach.
