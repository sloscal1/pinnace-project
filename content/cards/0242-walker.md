## Biography

He made one appearance for the full Scottish side, amongst other minor league representative matches, against Wales a 2-1 win.

Following some months out of the game, in 1926 he made a come back, with Third Lanark, where he struggled with fitness, leading to a career ending broken leg. Following Football he worked as a taxation officer in Edinburgh, until his death at the age of 52 from complications, following stomach surgery.
