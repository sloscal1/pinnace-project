## Biography

*In 1921 Rotherham County merged to form Rotherham Town, before later becoming Rotherham United, some of Lounds apps were for the team after merging.

**That season Leeds City folded, due to financial issues, and resigned their league status. On 17 October 1919, an auction was held at the Metropole Hotel in Leeds, where the playing staff of Leeds was auctioned off along with other assets of the club. The 16 members of the playing squad were bought by 9 different clubs for a total of £9,250 (£397,000+ now).

Lounds was purchased for the princely sum of £250 (£10,000+ now)
