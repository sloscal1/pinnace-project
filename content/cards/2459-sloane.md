## Biography

Sloane, a former school teacher, was a safe and neat goalkeeper, playing regularly until replaced byPeter Floyd.

With chances restricted by Floyd he moved back to Morecombe, where he had initially started his football journey.

At he assisted Morcombe to win the Lancashire Junior Cup/ in 1924/25 season.
