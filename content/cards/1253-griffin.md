## Biography

Ebbw Vale

Newport born left back Jimmy Griffin began his football career with Oak Villa in 1918 before joining hometown Southern League club Newport County in the 1919 close season. A regular in his first season at Somerton Park, when he played 42 League and Cup matches, he was a regular member of County's team in their inaugural Football League season after they joined the Third Division (South) at the start of the 1920-21. In their first three League seasons he made a further 85 appearances for The club before joining Second Division Stockport County in May 1923. However he played only twice for The Hatters at the start of the 1923-24 season before losing his place, returning to Wales to join Ebbw Vale in 1924, where he was a member of their 1926 Welsh Cup Final side that beat Swansea Town, the only time in the club's history that they have won the Welsh Cup.
