## Biography

Former schoolbay international and one of many Newport captures from the north eastern area.

He was polished and resourceful full back, who loved getting forward and was accomplished instigator of the offside game.

Thanks to 'Ironsides, A lifetime in the league, Who's Who of Newport County' by Tony Ambrosen
