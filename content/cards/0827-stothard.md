## Biography

Benwell, Newcastle born right or centre half Edward Stothard played for Scotswood in 1918 before signing for newly elected Second Division club South Shields in July 1919, making his Football League debut at Fulham that August in their inaugural League fixture. He made 16 appearances during the first half of his debut season, exactly half of his 32 appearances for South Shields, the latter 4 of which were at centre half, but during his four further seasons with Shields, he only saw occasional first team action, mainly at centre half, before joining non league West Stanley in 1924.

During WW1 he served with the 1st Northumberland Ambulance (RAMC)

He later worked for Armstrong Whitworth & Co, Scotswood, Shipping, Aeronautical and automotive engineers.
