## Biography

Duckett was an English footballer who played for Bradford City, Halifax Town and Bradford Park Avenue. He was the nephew of Horace Duckett, 1867-1939 who played international rugby for England. He captained City throughout the 1920's.

Obviously Duckett was born way before the inception of the cartoon character (Donald Duck) I am sure had he been christened much later then he really would have drawn the brunt of childhood ridicule, and football crowd humour.
