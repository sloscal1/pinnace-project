## Biography

Although Ronald's record shows 31 league and cup appearances scoring no goals, it would appear that those stats refer to his time playing in the Football League, however, it appears he was with Watford from around 1914 in the Southern League where he appeared many more times scoring a large amount of goals and won Southern League Championship with them, in 1915.

He arrived at Forest in 1921 on the reputation forged at Watford, arriving for a fee of £500 (£20,000+ now) but he failed to impress, in his two years at the club, making only 4 appearances. In 1923 he returned to non-league football in the North East.
