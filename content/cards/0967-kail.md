## Biography

It seems Kail started his career in football whist combining it with other work, given his playing details and date of birth he started playing regular football at the age of 15 and its known that he made at least 21 appearances at amateur level for England and 3 appearances for the full England team, being the last one league player but not the last amateur to represent a full English International team.

He represented the full England side during a 1929 European tour, in friendly fixtures v France 9th May scoring twice in a 4-1 win, v Belgium 11th of May a 5-1 win and v Spain a 4-3 defeat in Madrid.
