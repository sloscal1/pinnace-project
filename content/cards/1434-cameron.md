## Biography

Very little written or known about Cameron, he was obviously well travelled, possibly for working reasons, with many still trying to find their way and make ends meet following WW1. Its obvious he trod the path of many a footballing adventurer and went to play in the newly formed (edition one) of the American Soccer League, but its not known if he stayed in America following retirement from active football.
