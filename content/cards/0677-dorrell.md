## Biography

England 1924-1925 4 apps 1 goal

First app v Belgium Dec 8th 1924 4-0 win at the Hawthorns West Bromwich.

Last app v Ireland Oct 25th 1925 0-0 draw at Windsor Park Belfast.

He is credited with one goal for England against France, a friendly, in Paris a 3-2 win May 21st 1925.

Dorrell played for both Carey Hill & R.A.S.C. as a youth before joining Villa in May 1919.

He became a regular player and scorer for Villa right up to 1931 where upon he signed for Port Vale following former Villa player Richard York to Vale the same year.

On retirement he returned to Villa as a coach
