## Biography

Described as being tall and rangy, making him perfect build for his position, he could equally attack and defend well, and was plucked from his home village team, Seaham Harbour, by Manchester City.

In his six years at City he could not break into the first team.

The War intervened and after demobilization he joined Forest becoming a main stay of the defence until injury forced his absence from the team, and he lost his place to Gerry Morgan.

Parker guested for Port Vale, Stoke, and Chesterfield during WW1.
