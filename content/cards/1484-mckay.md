## Biography

Arrived at the club in 1921 from Glasgow Celtic with Archie Longmuir, (page 87 Card 1117). Described as a skillful arty, chance maker and seen as a key part of Blackburn's post war re build. Despite mesmerising dribbling skills and spectacular goals, McKay was unable to maintain consistency for team or individual success. Capped only once by Scotland, a tendency towards showmanship, probably cost him a prolonged run in the national team. Entrusted with penalty taking duties he made each penalty into his own piece of theatre. Leaving Blackburn for Middlesbrough for £3750 (£184,000+ now) he finished his days there and had a brief spell, back in Scotland, with Hibernian.
