| 1920-1924 | Luton Town | 130 | 6 | Alf started out at Peterborough City before moving to West Ham in 1913. Although not a regular for the Hammers, Alf missed a chunk of his football career due to the Great War and came to Luton in the summer of 1920. A left back who was said to be 'hefty' he was a good tackler who read the game well. A virtual ever-present for three years he was badly injured early in his fourth season and decided to call it a day and retire to concentrate on his business interests. Alf passed away in 1944.
