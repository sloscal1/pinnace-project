## Biography

Pre and post football he was an ironmonger and also worked in his father's trade of painter and decorator. In World War I he served with the Royal Army Service Corps and was later a local councillor for the Liberal Party in his hometown of Southport.

It is unknown why Watson did not use his Christian name, given him at birth.
