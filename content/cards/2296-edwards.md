## Biography

Edwards played for Wales in an amateur international match against England Amateurs on the 24th January1920 in Merthyr. According to the Daily Express report, "E.J. Edwards, the Merthyr outside left, was the star artist of the Welsh attack, and his elusive runs and centres were a feature of the first half, but following change of ends he was kept well under control"; his team lost 9–0.
