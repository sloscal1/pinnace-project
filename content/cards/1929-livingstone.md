## Biography

Teams Managed : 1949-50 Sparta Rotterdam, 1951-53 Rep of Ireland, 1953-54 Belgium, 1954-56 Newcastle Utd, 1956-58 Fulham, 1958-1962 Chesterfield.

Guided Belgium to the 1954 World Cup, which saw a trilling 4-4 draw with England, June 17th, 1954, Basle, Switzerland.

Guided Newcastle Utd to the 1954 FA cup final. The Newcastle board had the final say in which players would be playing during his spell at the club and they notably played Jackie Milburn for this final, against Livingstone's wishes. Milburn coincidentally scored in the match.

It until 1was largely believed that Livingstone was unhappy with the amount of influence the board had on first team issues (although he never stated this). He left Newcastle in 1956 and went on to manage Fulham between 1956 and 1958 and then Chesterfield 1962.
