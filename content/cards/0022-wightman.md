## Biography

Between 1927 and 1939 Wightman became an eminent football manager and coach, managing Derby as assistant 1927-29, Notts Co 1930-31 as a coach, Luton Town 1931-35, Mansfield Town 1936 and latterly Nottingham Forest 1936-39.
