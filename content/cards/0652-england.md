## Biography

England was a major component of the early 1920's Sunderland team, always stern faced and serious, he was described as resolute, busy full back who 'took no prisoners' strong in the tackle the perfect foil to the cool Warney Cresswell (1897-1973)(see page 865 Card 76), playing on the other defensive flank.

He gave Sunderland 10 years dedicated service leaving for an ill fated 6 months at West Ham where he experienced the misery of being away from his family. He contemplated retirement but newly promoted to the Football League, Mansfield Town, came to the rescue offering a lifeline which he accepted. He later had a brief dalliance with Frickley Colliery where he became club Captain.
