## Biography

Outside right Edwin "Teddy" Brown started his football career with Tranent Juniors, from where he joined Heart of Midlothian in December 1920, making his Scottish League debut in a 2-1 win over Airdrieonians in March 1921. He made just a single appearance per season for Hearts that season and one more the following season when he played in a defeat at Albion Rovers in March 1922. He moved to Raith Rovers in May 1922 and made 3 appearances for Raith during 1922-23. His career thereafter is uncertain
