## Biography

Known as 'Monty' he served on a mine sweeper during WW1.

Following the war and returning to Ashington for the 1920-21 season, he found Pat O'Connell (1887-1959) a seasoned International but nearing the end of his career, had been brought into the Ashington squad and Hopper transferred to Lincoln.

Millwall quickly showed interest in his skills and he was quickly transferred to them for a fee of £550 (£20,000+ now).
