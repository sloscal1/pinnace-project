## Biography

Joining West Brom for a fee of £100 ( £9,000+ now) from Brierley Hill in 1911, he soon became renown as a brilliantly Consistent, right winger, full of dash, vigour, and possessing pace.

He broke his leg in 1922 effectively ending his career.
