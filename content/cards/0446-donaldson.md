## Biography

Slightly built but described as a 'canny' winger, he specialised in a fine body swerve and accurate crosses.

Following WW1 where he worked in a munitions factory he was plagued with injury culminating in a badly shattered knee cap which led to the end of league football. He recovered well enough to play lower grade football, namely in the Lancashire Combination, playing into his 40's.

After football he became a publican.
