## Biography

England 1910 to 1920 6 apps 1 goal, First cap v Ireland February 12th 1-1 draw Solitude Stadium, Belfast Ireland.

Last cap v Ireland October 23rd 1920 2-0 win Home Championship Roker Park Sunderland Managed Fulham 1924 to 1926

Ducat also excelled as a cricketer playing for Surrey and England, alongside Jack Hobbs, Tom Hayward, and Ernest Hayes.

A fine batsman and bowler Ducat played in one test series, the third, at Headingley against Australia in 1921.

Plagued throughout his sporting life with injury, enduring a broken leg in 1912 and broken arm in 1924, its possible his sporting success could have been far greater without these incumbencies.

He was featured in the 1920 edition of The Wisden Cricket Almanac, as being one of the Cricketers of the year.

Ironically Ducat died whilst playing in a wartime Cricket match, for his unit side, against a team from the Home Guard, at Lords Cricket ground, London.
