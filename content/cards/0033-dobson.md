## Biography

Dobson also "guested" for Stoke during World War I

The picture at left here is an 'error' card.; whilst it is correct in so far as Dobson did indeed play for Barnsley (1919-20), he has been mistakenly named George William Cope Donkin, another Barnsley player who appears separately on card 594. There are two other cards numbered 33 (see right) which use this same picture but correctly identify him as George Dobson of Norwich City. [Roger Pashby]
