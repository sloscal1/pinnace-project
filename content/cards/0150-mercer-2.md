## Biography

Originally working as a Coal miner, he began his career in non-League football with Prescot Athletic, St Helens Town and Skelmersdale United, Mercer joined Second Division club Hull City for a £40 (£3,000+ now) fee on 22 January 1914. He made 91 league appearances and scored 26 goals for the club before moving to First Division Sheffield United on 13 December 1920, for a British record transfer fee of £4,500 (£168,000+ now). He made 241 league and cup appearances and scored 22 goals for the Bramall Lane club and won the 1924/25 FA Cup, before leaving in 1928.

Aside from a spell with Torquay United during the 1929/30 season, Mercer played the remainder of his career in non-League football.

Mercer represented the Football League XI and won two caps for England, the first October 21st 1922 v Ireland, a 2-0 win at The Hawthorne's, West Bromwich, the second, scoring once, in a 6–1 victory over Belgium on 19 March 1923, at Highbury, London.

Mercer was the older brother of fellow footballer Arthur Stanley Mercer (1902-1994) and the pair briefly played together with the Blades, while his other brother Richard (no details) was an amateur footballer. David's son, Arthur Davis Mercer (1918-1986), also known as David, also became a professional footballer. In 1911, Mercer was working in a coal mine and in 1939 he was working as a golf club groundsman.
