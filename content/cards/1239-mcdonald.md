## Biography

Lovell's Athletic

As can be seen from the card, his surname has been misspelt, possibly occurring on an occasion where the collection was re printed.

Roy was an amateur, playing at least once, for the Welsh national side, at amateur level.

He was a regular goalscorer for County's reserve side during 1922/23 season making only 9 first team appearances.

Joining Lovell's, a Newport based confectionary maker, it seems, he liked to combine his football, with a normal everyday work routine, he later became their manager from 1939 to 1945.

He was employed at Lovell's as a buyer, it is known that he also became a Southern League committee member.
