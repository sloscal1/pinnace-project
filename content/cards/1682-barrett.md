## Biography

Initially it was presumed he was possibly the brother of Arthur and Albert, but it was unusual that nothing could be found relating to a playing record, or even his first names, until stumbling on an obscure web site which referenced this character, William Frederick, and displaying a picture taken from a team group, which bared a remarkable resemblance to the image in the Pinnace collection Checks on Ancestry.com have 99% confirmed that William is probably the cousin of Arthur and Albert, three sons of two brothers who worked in the printing trade around London.

For some unknown reason he played his football in France for some eight years, and was probably in or around the Leytonstone team at the time these images for the cards were taken.
