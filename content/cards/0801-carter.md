## Biography

Not only an accomplished wing half, in football, Carter played Cricket for Derbyshire, still playing in 1939.

He became a Cricket professional in Scotland coaching at Repton School, before becoming a football coach and caretaker at Callowland School in Watford.

Ironically, Carter died in Shrodells Hospital (now Watford General) next door to Watford's Vicarage Road ground.
