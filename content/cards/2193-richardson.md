## Biography

Stockport County's right full back for five seasons, Billy Richardson was totally reliable and consistent defender, strong in the tackle with good powers of recovery if or when beaten.

He made 25 appearances in his first season, qualifying him for a championship medal as Stockport won the Third Division (North) in 1921/22 season, the defence conceding only 21 goals in 38 league games that season.

Leaving Stockport in the close season of 1925/26 season there was a brief stay at Poole FC, their first in the southern league, which included a memorable run in the FA cup, being knocked out at the third round, 3-1, by Division one, Everton.

Another short stay at Ashington was followed by a brief move to Durham City, making 14 appearances. There he was cast straight into a relegation battle, ending with relegation and the clubs, failure for re-election. His high light from his stay at Durham must surely be his only goal, a 3-2 win, scoring from the penalty spot against Bradford City.

Latterly he returned to Ashington where he made 10 more appearances before dropping back into none league football, possibly combining football with work in the local collieries of County Durham.
