## Biography

Later became reserve team coach at Wigan. His two brothers Robert, 1884-? and Duncan 1892-1916 played professional football. Duncan was shot in the right shoulder in no-man's land and died instantly during an assault on La Boisselle. He is commemorated on the Thiepval Memorial.
