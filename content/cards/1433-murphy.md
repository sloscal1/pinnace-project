## Biography

His parents had yet to marry when he was born, and the certificate bears his mother's maiden surname of Elliot. He lived with his maternal great-aunt in childhood, and on two editions of the census her married name Dickie was given as John's surname (it was also his middle name). He later used his father's surname Murphy. He was a winger, usually deployed on the right.
