## Biography

Initially played for several clubs prior to joining Villa, notably New Delaval Villa, West Stanley, Blyth Spartans & Durham City England 1 app 0 goals 1924. v Wales, Home International, 1st may 1924, 2-1 defeat at Ewood Park Blackburn.

He was brought to the club to eventually replace an aging but still competent Harry Hampton, apparently lacking pace he was gifted and intuitive passer of the ball, his passes described by some " as sweet as a stolen kiss ", in fact he played alongside Hampton in the 1913 FA Cup win.

Early in his Villa tenure he was loaned to Stourbridge from 1910 to 1911.

Surprisingly in 1921 he left Villa joining a successful Huddersfield Town team where he became integral to their Championship wins of 1924 to 1926 and being part of their 1922 FA cup winning team.

Surprisingly he only won one England car two less than is lesser formidable brother George, (see page 10).

Clem retired in may 1929 becoming manager at Huddersfield Town a position he held for 13 years leading them to success in the 1930 and 1938 cup final.
