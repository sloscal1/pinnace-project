## Biography

Also on the books of Durham City in the same period for this card set, was Thomas 'Tom' Davison 1901 in Edmondsley, an Inside Forward who made 18 appearances scoring 4 goals for the club. A comparison of a photograph entered onto the English National Football Archive (ENFA) site, infers that the picture is actually Thomas Reay Davison.
