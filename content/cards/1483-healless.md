## Biography

England 1924-1928 2 appearances, first v Ireland 22nd October 1924 Home Championship, 3-1 win Goodison Park Liverpool. last app v Scotland 31st March 1928 Home Championship Wembley Stadium, London.

Described as 'lightweight' build, not particularly skillful, but a tireless worker, capable of filling all defensive roles. Strong in the tackle and a useful header of the ball. Having an ability to organise his playing colleagues in the field of play he became a natural candidate for captaincy. Capped twice by England his proudest moment was lifting the FA cup as winners v Huddersfield in 1928. Retiring at nearly 40 years of age he turned to coaching at varying clubs and abroad in Holland, returning to coach Blackburn he moved aside when Johnny Carey came to manage Blackburn. He was still playing at amateur level at the age of 51.
