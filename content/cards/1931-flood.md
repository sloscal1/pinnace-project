## Biography

Flood was discovered by the then Plymouth manager, Robert Jack, playing for the army in the Royal Garrison Artillery team, where he had scored 30 goals in just 8 games!

Flood also aspired as a useful Cricketer, being a handy batsman and wicket keeper, associated with both Nottinghamshire and DEvon County Cricket Club's. He later played for Hull Cricket Club, and in 1963 was appointed their coach.
