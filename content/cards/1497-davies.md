## Biography

Marine engineer before football, and was as a member of the famous 1927 Cardiff City FA cup winning team. Played Cricket for Glamorgan.

Following Football retirement Davies became a school sports teacher in Cheshire and subsequently worked on aircraft production leading into WW2.
