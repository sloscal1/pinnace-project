## Biography

Frederick "Jerry" Morgan played for Bristol Rovers between 1919 and 1925, having previously played 2 games for Bristol City. He was part of a large footballing family from Barton Hill in Bristol. His brother Jim (no record) played for Wolves, and brother Thomas 1886, played for Bristol City twice in 1912-13 season. His nephew William James Morgan 1922-1975 also made 104 appearances for Bristol Rovers between 1946 and 1952. Jerry Morgan scored two hat tricks for Rovers, one against Worksop Town in a famous 9-0 cup victory in 1920, and the other against Norwich in 1923.

He served in the Royal Navy during World War I as a stoker on board the dreadnought HMS Marlborough. He died of throat cancer in Bristol in 1953.
