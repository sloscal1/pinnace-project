## Biography

A player with which little has been remembered, seems he was in lower league football other than a foray with Watford for whom he never made a 1st team appearance and was at Barrow at a time the Pinnace Collection was being prepared.

He later played for Mold Town in 1923, although the date might be debatable as Mold Town did not form until 1929. Scotswood 1924, an anonymous team probably from the Scotswood area of Newcastle, given that Hardy was from that area originally and then a blank until 1929 when research finds him playing for Vono Sports, believed to be a team in the West Midlands, then much later in1934 for Truro City. It would be fair to assume that Hardy may well have been journeyman part timer playing for clubs wherever his true profession took him.

It is presumed that he died in Wellington, England as there is no inference of emigrating to New Zealand
