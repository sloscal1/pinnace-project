## Biography

A strong powerful full back, operating in a successful Queens Park team of the 1920's.

When WW1 broke out he signed up with the Gordon Highlanders, rising to the rank of Captain and seeing action on the Western Front, being wounded, and later seeing service in India.

Returning from the war he studied at Aberdeen University where he not only played football but also represented the college at Tennis and Golf. He was still a student when he signed for Queens Park. Capped twice by the full Scotland squad in 1926 and 1930 he played once for the Scottish XI and six times for the Scottish amateur side.

Still an amateur, he worked in Dumbarton, until he gave up playing in 1930, taking a post on county council roads department, in Banff, Aberdeenshire. During WW2 he was a Major in the Royal Engineers finding and training recruits for engineering projects, and, following the war he returned to Banff where he became county surveyor retiring in 1962, to Edinburgh, where he died in 1981.
