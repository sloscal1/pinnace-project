## Biography

Kenny worked as a shoemaker. In February 1915, six months since the outbreak of the First World War, he enlisted as a private in the Football Battalion of the Middlesex Regiment. He was wounded in 1916 and emigrated to Canada after the war, although his playing record infers his move to Canada was much later. His record infers he played for Nanaimo City in British Columbia but the only record of a team in that city is an American rules Football team, Vancouver Island Raiders.
