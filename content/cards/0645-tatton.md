## Biography

Started his career with home town side Dunston Atlas Villa, in Gateshead, before having a short time, as an amateur, with Newcastle United, without making an appearance. Moving on to Gillingham, it was a similar situation there, before moving to Preston North End and making 18 appearance before football was curtailed by WW1. Following the war he signed for Oldham Athletic, making 70 appearances before, it is thought, his career was cut short by injury. Described as fast and tricky left winger with an ability to deliver good crosses to his inside player.
