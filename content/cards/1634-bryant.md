## Biography

England 1 appearance 21st May 1923 v France 3-2 win, a friendly played in Paris, France.

After asking Clapton for a trial as an inside Forward, Clapton converted him to that of a useful Centre Half, later becoming one of the best centre backs in the country, despite still being of amateur status, and also becoming club captain. He had several seasons at Millwall before returning to Clapton prior to retirement. It appears he won the FA amateur cup in 1923-24 and 1924/25 seasons and the Football League (south) title, with Millwall in 1927/28 season. He also won a winners medal in the FA Charity Shield in 1925 and 1926, playing for the amateurs.

He was a useful cricketer playing for With CC and Old Ovarians.

Although born in Belgium Bryant probably qualified to play for England due to parentage or simply long term residency in the UK.
