## Biography

Bought by West Ham, from Southend United, for £600 ( £26,000+ now) in 1919, Jack was described as a dashing winger. who converted to full back, Transferring to Queens Park Rangers in 1926, he became something of a penalty expert scoring 12 times from the spot.

His later career at West Ham and later at QPR was blighted by ill health and on retirement became a scout under former Hammers player George Kay (Card659.See page 940).

In retirement he kept a small farm in the Whitburn area of County Durham

F.A.Cup runners up 1923.
