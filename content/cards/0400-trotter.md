## Biography

Born in Easington, County Durham, Trotter served in the Royal Garrison Artillery during the First World War. His professional career began with Bury from where he joined The Wednesday in 1921. He scored 109 goals in 154 League matches, including five in one game against Portsmouth in December 1924. During his tenure at The Wednesday he was the leading goal scorer of the Second Division in the season 1925–26 and in the First Division in the season 1926–27. During both seasons he scored 37 goals in the league.

Trotter managed Charlton Athletic between 1956 and 1961 with moderate success.
