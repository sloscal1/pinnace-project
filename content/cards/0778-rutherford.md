## Biography

Rutherford was born in Bedlington, Northumberland, in 1894. He played for Ashington of the Northern Alliance before turning professional with Brighton & Hove Albion of the newly formed Football League Third Division. His older brother John 'Jack' Rutherford1892-1930, was already on Brighton's books, and the pair played in the same team in 15 competitive matches. After losing his place to new signing Jack Feerberry (card 53, page 125) in 1921, Rutherford spent the 1921–22 season in the reserves and left the club in September 1922. He moved to the United States where he died in 1924.
