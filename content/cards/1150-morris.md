## Biography

Davie Morris was the son a merchant seaman and worked as a shipwright prior to a good career in football.

He played 6 times for the Scottish national team, his first game v Ireland in 1923 and his last v Wales in 1925, captaining Scotland in most of his appearances and during their successful, Home Championship campaign of 1925.

He was also selected to represent the Scottish league on one occasion

He was transferred to Preston in 1925 for a fee of £4,200 ( £210,000+ now), and captained them as he had at Raith.

By 1930 he was out of first team contention, being placed, on the transfer list, but an extortionate asking price reduced interest from other clubs, forcing him to play in none league for Chester until his contract ran out.

Following his brief encounter at Chester, he returned to play in Scotland.
