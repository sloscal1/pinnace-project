## Biography

Described as a cool calculating player who specialised in close passing, which was so indicative of the Scottish game at that time. Not a prolific scorer but was a creator of goals for other and a mainstay of the 1923 Championship winning team.

Following that season he became marginalised in selection, which, due to his popularity with the crowd, drew heated letters of complaint to the directors of Nelson Football Club. Honours : Nelson : Third Division Championship 1922-23 Thanks to Nelson in the Football League, An who's who by Garth Dykes
