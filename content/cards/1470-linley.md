## Biography

Linley was born in East Retford, Nottinghamshire, described as a rather unlikely looking footballer, balding, and knock kneed, never the less, 'Raz' Lindley could show his heels to most full backs of his time. He played football for Kiveton Park and Midland League club Worksop Town before signing for Birmingham in December 1920 for a fee reported as £800 plus a player. He helped them win the Football League Second Division title in the 1920–21 season and appeared in more than 100 top-flight matches for the club. He also played for Nottingham Forest, Sutton Town, Mansfield Town, and Shirebrook.
