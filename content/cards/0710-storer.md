## Biography

It appears that Storer started his career with Gresley Rovers and was then an inside forward as opposed to other statistical records inferring he was a centre half.

He was sold to Bradford City for a reported £150 (£13,000 now) including 2 other players. During the war he spent much time back in Leicestershire, guesting for Leicester Fosse and Coalville Swifts.
