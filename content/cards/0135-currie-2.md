## Biography

A winner of many trophies whilst playing for Kilwinning Rangers, his move to Leicester Fosse cost him a medal as he played in the drawn game of the Scottish Junior cup and was elsewhere when the replay was played which Kilwinning actually won.

At Leicester he accrued 248 league and cup appearances plus another 123 during WW1 staying long enough to be awarded a benefit game in November 1919
