## Biography

Joining Nelson prior to the football league, Bob had a bad start experiencing recovery from a broken nose and subsequent surgery, prior to the 1920-21 season. Described as fearless in the tackle self assured in his clearances, he was adept at recovering should players pass him. Was unfortunate to experience another serious injury in October 1923 not fully recovering until April 1924. His summer months were spent behind the stumps playing cricket in the Lancashire cricket league.
