## Biography

A capable defender but with a tendency to hold the ball too long. Experiencing surgery for knee problems he lost his place to Clem Rigg (above) but regained a place in the team, more as a utility player, later regaining the left back birth. Later associated with Athurlie and unsuccessfully trialing at Arsenal, finishing his days at Brentford.
