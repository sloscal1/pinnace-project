## Biography

Some confusion over this character, some references give his name as just Percival Dawson, 29th of November 1890 in Cullercoats, Scotland, whilst he played his early football in the north east where Cullercoats is also referenced, but all show the same player picture. It seems his transfer to Blackburn was a record at the time, £2500 (£200,000+ today) and it is thought he played War time Internationals for Scotland. It does seem that he served in the McCrea's Battalion during WW1*, which fitted the criteria at the time as he was playing for Hearts at the outbreak of war.
