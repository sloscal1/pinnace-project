## Biography

By the time of his death in November 1924 he had scored 10 goals in 75 appearances, and played all but 2 games that season, however he tragically died as a result of injuries sustained in a collision with Coventry City's goalkeeper. After playing in the 2-0 home win, Harvey returned to his home in Watford but on complaining of stomach pains was admitted to hospital. Diagnosed as having a burst blood vessel he sadly died the following Wednesday.
