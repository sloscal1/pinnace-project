## Biography

Oxley's brother William 'Billy' (1899-1951) was professional footballer .

Dick was a well-known professional sprinter who won the Powderhall Handicap. His private life was dogged by tragedy; his wife died in 1927, just 5 years after their marriage, and he returned home to Wallsend. Their Only son, Jack, died of consumption at 20 (1943) while on an officer cadet course. Within seven years, Dick himself had died of jaundice.
