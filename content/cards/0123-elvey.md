## Biography

A poor referenced player. Bolton paid a then club record of £2,500 (£91,000+ now) for him, later taking a loss, selling to Arsenal for £250 in 1922. In 1920, he also toured with the FA party in South Africa and was an unused member of the British football team in 1902 Olympics.
