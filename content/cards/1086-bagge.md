## Biography

Harry Bagge was an English professional footballer who made over 170 appearances in the Football League for Fulham as a wing half. He He was described as "a consistent player" who feeds his forwards with discrimination Later played for, and managed Spanish club, Athletic Bilbao, and subsequently Salamanca and RB Linense.

In December 1915, over a year after Britain's entry into the First World War, Bagge attested in the Royal Naval Air Service. He served as an Air Mechanic 2nd Class through the war and later in the Royal Air Force, before being transferred to the RAF Reserve in March 1919.[
