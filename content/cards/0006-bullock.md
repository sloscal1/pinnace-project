## Biography

London Combination: 1918–1 (as a WW1 guest 29 apps between 1916-1919)

Bullock won one cap for England, which came in a 2–0 win over Ireland in 1920. He won an amateur cap in 1910. He served as a lance corporal in the Football Battalion during the First World War and was wounded in the right shoulder during the Battle of the Somme in 1916, in the region of Delville Wood and Guillemont. He was injured in the left knee after an accident in 1918 and was demobilised in March 1920. After his retirement from football in 1922, Bullock became landlord of the Slubber's Arms pub in Huddersfield. He died of heart failure due to ammonia poisoning in November 1922 and had been suffering "nerve troubles" during the month preceding his death.
