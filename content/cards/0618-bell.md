## Biography

Tough Tackling full back, visually notable for his shock of ginger hair, he was also a competent goalkeeper, captaining Hull for the last four seasons of his tenure. Reluctantly he left the club for Forest seeing out his career there and becoming part of the coaching staff before coaching in Holland where he would have to flee the country to avoid the Nazi's.
