## Biography

Whilst only 20/21 years of age in 1921 it seems McFerran was possibly a fringe player at Darlington, having more interests outside of football. He became a marine engineer, and in 1952, he, his wife and their son emigrated to Canada.

McFerran died in London, Ontario, in 1996 and was buried there in Forest Lawn Memorial Gardens.
