## Biography

Another player blighted by WW1 and seems to have had an intermittent association with the none league being a member of teams at Horwich RMI at varying times throughout his career. On conclusion of his professional playing days at New Brighton he returned to none league football with Horwich RMI in 1926, also playing subsequently for Rosendale United in 1929 and Burscough Rangers in 1930 before a fourth and final spell with Horwich RMI in 1931 before his eventual retirement.
