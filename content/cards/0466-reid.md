## Biography

Born and bred in the Baillieston area of Glasgow Reid played his early football for junior league side Baillieston Thistle.

Although much of his playing CV is a little vague, during his time at Third Lanark he was in the team that were beaten finalists in the Scottish FA cup final losing to Hearts in 1906.

Everywhere Reid played he became a prolific goalscorer never more so than when joined Glasgow Rangers, where he won three Scottish league championships scoring 188 goals throughout his eleven years with the Glasgow giants.

He was top league scorer in 1910/11 and 1911/12 seasons

Between 1910 and 1916 Reid played 9 times for the full Scottish national side scoring 4 goals, his first cap being v Wales and his last v England. Prior to that he had represented the Scottish league select XI on 8 occasions scoring 10 goals representing both the select team and the full Scottish sides once on separate occasions during the First World War in 1916.

During WW1 Reid was attached as a gunner to the Royal Field Artillery, 52nd Lowland Division Reid is ranked 4th in the list of all time Scottish scorers with 275 career goals
