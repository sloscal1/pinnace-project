## Biography

Another early 1900's football journeymen, those moving around to establish theirselves financially whilst still relishing their love of football in post WW1 Britain, He was influential enough for Manchester United, in 1921, to pay £750 (£30,000+ now) for his services.

Whilst Scott didn't have a really illustrious playing career, in terms of honours in the game, he certainly played in influential areas of the game, even trying his hand for a couple of years in the newly formed American Soccer League, (ASL).

There is very little written about him, an open search on the internet infers he was a WW1 hero earning a Military Medal, whilst The English National Footballers Archive (ENFA), shows him as a MA, Master of Arts?, but, it seems, no one, has an inkling, what if any of that, is true.
