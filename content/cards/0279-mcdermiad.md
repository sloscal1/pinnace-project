## Biography

McDermiad was described as an hard working talented inside forward, being an important factor in Aberdeen's successful league title challenge of 1929/30 season, captaining the side and becoming a father figure in a very young side.

He retired in 1935 and became assistant trainer to Donald Coleman, during the war, and then in his own right until ill health forced retirement in February 1952. He died six moths later. Thanks to Aberdeen a Complete Record 1903/1987 by Jim Rickaby
