## Biography

Finlay was selected once for the Scottish Football League XI in 1909, and in 1920 played in the Home Scots v Anglo-Scots international trial match, but he never received a full cap for Scotland.

Joined Newcastle United for a fee of £775 ( £76,000+ now).

Following retirement became trainer at St James Park until 1930.

* These two cards are the same but one is incorrectly spelt for the period of the collection.

However, there was a John Grey Findlay (1883-1916) also known as 'Jock', playing at Newcastle, making 2 appearances in 1905-1906, who was killed in France during WW1.
