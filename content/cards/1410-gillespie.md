## Biography

A natural leader, epitomizing the amateur game in Scotland in the inter war years.

Brought up on the southside of Glasgow playing his early years with Queens Park Strollers and Battlefield Juniors..

A one club man, playing mostly at Centre Half but called upon, occasionally at Centre Forward.

He won eleven amateur caps and made four appearances for the full squad , captaining Scotland at both levels at differing points in time..

Following retirement he became club secretary, at Queens Park, until 1940, and later President. He was a partner in a Glasgow chartered accountancy company and served as a JP. Thanks to 'The men who made Scotland ' by Andy Mitchell
