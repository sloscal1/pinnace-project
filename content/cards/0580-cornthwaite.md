## Biography

Tom Cornthwaite started his Football career playing for Bradford City but didn't actually make an appearance for first team, joining, Fleetwood in 1912 for a brief spell, before joining Preston North End where, again, he did not break into the first team.

Suspension of Football due to the intervention of WW1, meant, that on his return to football, he was 29 when he joined Bury, possibly his best years being lost, like many fellow footballers of this period, to the Great War. He became their first choice keeper, debuting against Wolves on October the 11th1919.

Besides being a Footballer, Cornthwaite was a part time Policeman, leading to him missing several games when a Miners strike occurred and all Police leave was cancelled.

Dropping from the professional game into none league from 1926 to 1931 he played for several clubs including, Connah's Quay & Shotton, Lytham, Manchester North End and Crosland & Pickstone Ltd, the latter possibly being work related.
