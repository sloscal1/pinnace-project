## Biography

Prior to to Football, Roberts was employed as a Drapery Wharehouseman.

On the outbreak of WW1, he joined, and fought with Royal Garrison Artillery, being mentioned in dispatches and receiving the Military Medal (MM) for heroic conduct, following serious injury, at the Battle of the Somme, in 1916.
