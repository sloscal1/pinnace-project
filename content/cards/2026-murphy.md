## Biography

An anomaly for the reader over the true identity of this card image.

According to 'Celtic Wikipedia' a web site dedicated to Glasgow Celtic the image and the above details to go together.

But during the same period the following was present at the club and could cast doubt as to who in fact is shown in the card image.

According to Scottish League Payers Records1890to1939 by Steve Emms & Richard Wells published by Soccer date James I Murphy 25th February 1902 Hamilton, Scotland, was also present at Celtic between 1921 and 1923 making only 4 recorded appearances

It seems this player, another football nomad roaming the leagues of football throughout the UK in the early 1920's, made numerous appearances at Ayr Utd, Clydebank, Mid Rhondda, all on loan, supposedly from Celtic, and making 17 appearances for Charlton Athletic in 1926-1927 season, which appears to have been a permanent contract, before leaving them for Merthyr Town and latterly Morton, in Scotland.

Its possible, but no proof, as there is no reference to either's middle name initial, that the initials were used by statisticians and Celtic to differentiate between the two player profiles. Unfortunately there is nothing to connect the two players directly to one another, as possible siblings.
