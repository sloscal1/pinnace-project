## Biography

Yews joined West Ham United for a fee of £150 (£7,000+ today) in 1923 and played in the high-scoring Hammers team that included Vic Watson, and Jimmy Ruffell on the opposite wing, it seems he was not present at the club or was not selected to play in the 1923, inaugural Wembley Cup final of that year. He left West Ham in 1933 and played three League games for Clapton Orient before retiring, and entering the motor trade, working for Briggs motor bodies before becoming a charge hand with the Ford motor company.
