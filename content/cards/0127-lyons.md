## Biography

Tommy Lyons was a fearless tackler, a full back who in his younger days was a Centre Half, with, Hednesford Town, Hednesford Victoria and Bridgtown Amateurs. He was signed by Aston Villa in 1907, following a successful trial. He developed into a good full back along side team mate Alfred 'Freddie' Miles (1884-1926), Lyons strength's, were his ability to cover his full back partner, and although being regarded as a hard man, he had an acute footballing brain.

Educated at Hazelsdale School, Hednesford, he played junior football for Heath Hayes Boys.

He died in 1938 after a short illness.
