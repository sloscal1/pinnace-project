## Biography

Kirk was one the game's real characters. Nicknamed 'Jazzo' he often played a piano, gave recitations, and did sleight of hand tricks after matches to entertain his fellow players!. In addition to the above stats, Kirk played briefly for Bath City in 1927 & 1930.

NB: in the photograph he is marked as an Exeter City player but it was taken while at Plymouth Argyle, whose shirt he wears.
