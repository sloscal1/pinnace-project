## Biography

McCrae had a brief loan spell in 1925 at Third Lanark whilst at Manchester Utd and finished his career in 1927-1928 season at Clyde playing 3 times and not scoring.

He later managed the Egypt National team from 1934 to 1936, overseeing them during the 1934 FIFA World Cup. His club management was spent at Istanbulspor in Turkey, and Fram in Iceland where he won the Icelandic league in 1947/47 season.
