## Biography

Millership played for Rochdale during WW1. He later ran a hotel in Castleford, Yorkshire and worked in the Winter Gardens, in Blackpool.

* That season Leeds City folded, due to financial issues, and resigned their league status. On 17 October 1919, an auction was held at the Metropole Hotel in Leeds, where the playing staff of Leeds was auctioned off along with other assets of the club. The 16 members of the playing squad were bought by 9 different clubs for a total of £9,250 (£397,000+ now). Millership was bought by Rotherham County for £1,000 (£42,000+ now), it was the second highest bid for a player. Billy McLeod (1887-1959) was bought for £1,250 (£53,000+ now) by Notts County.
