## Biography

Logan although, being small in stature, made up for that, with tremendous enthusiasm and the ability to attack. Some followers of Argyle felt that at times he neglected his defensive duties, occasionally getting caught forward, and out of position, others said that he should have been deployed, more regularly, further forward, as he was a better player, when attacking.

In 1925 he as awarded the proceeds from a game against south coast rivals Southampton as his benefit match.
