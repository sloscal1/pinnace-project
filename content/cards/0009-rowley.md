## Biography

Rowley spent some 47 years playing and coaching in the Football League.

He played for Oldham Athletic and Bolton Wanderers, spending 13 years with the latter club. After spending many years as part of Bolton's back-room staff, he was appointed as manager in August 1944. He spent six years in charge before retiring due to ill health. He coached Middlesbrough from June 1952 to February 1954, before, again stepping side, due to illness. His final management role was at Shrewsbury Town from July 1955 until June 1956.

Always a main stay of the Bolton first team Rowley missed the 1923 FA Cup Final against West Ham United because he was serving a six-week suspension for being sent-off against Huddersfield.

He managed Bolton, Middlesbrough, and latterly Shrewsbury Town between 1944 and 1957, much of his managerial career was blighted by illness.
