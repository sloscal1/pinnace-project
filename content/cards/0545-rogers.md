## Biography

ENFA, credits Rogers with only 142 appearances and 4 goals, whilst, www.townenders.com, a fan based Swindon Town dedicated stats site, credits him with 181 appearances and 30 goals, 30 goals alone, seems exorbitant for an half back, but, it appears during 1919/1920 season he scored 15 goals in 36 matches, leading to belief, that during that season he was deployed, in a more advanced position than half back.
