## Biography

Another player not well documented in club histories, again a little reliant on Michael Joyce's Football League Players Records, and Accrington Stanley a complete record, Breedon Books.

Richardson was viewed as a reliable, speedy, two footed, player.

He served in the Howitzer Brigade, at the rank of sergeant, during the war being awarded the Military Medal, whilst serving in France, during WW1.

Became a regular in the team his playing sequence rarely being effected by absence.

In 1924 Richardson was awarded a benefit game, reserves v Southport which was attended by 2,500 spectators, a crowd bigger than most league games.
