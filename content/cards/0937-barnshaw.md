## Biography

After starting his career at hometown club Hebburn Argyle Barnshaw moved to Sheffield Wednesday but an injury prevented him from ever playing in the first team. Barnshaw returned to Hebburn Argyle despite Wednesday retaining his registration. He eventually transferred to Sheffield United for a total of £90 (£8,000+ now) split between his two previous clubs.

After failing to make the break into the first team at Bramall Lane Barnshaw was sold to Watford after just one season for £50 (£4,000+ now). During his first season at Watford, he played 11 games during Watford's 1914/15 title-winning season. He also played as a guest for Leeds City and Darlington during the First World War, before finishing his career at Aberdare Athletic.
