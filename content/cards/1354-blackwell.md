## Biography

Despite being referenced in the collection with the title R. Blackwell, it does seem that he was called Harry, with no reference to Harry, being a nickname or part of a longer title.

Somewhat a journeyman. His time, being spent, in the background, at the clubs he played for.

However he appears to have made over 200 appearances for Aberdeen during a nine year stay before returning south.

An uneventful career is perhaps highlighted with an amusing anecdote where he apparently kept goal in the pouring rain in a 13-0 win over Peterhead, allegedly he wore a rain coat, at times sheltering under an umbrella, obviously a crowd pleasing joker, Blackwell's older brother Ernest, played as a Goalkeeper for both Scunthorpe & Lindsey Utd.

His cousin was the legendary goalkeeper Sam Hardy (see card No 4) in this collection, Hardy's profile is on page 6 of this book.
