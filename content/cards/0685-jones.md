## Biography

Difficult to establish if the picture and details appertain to the same. ENFA reference a player at Burnley at the correct time period for the card collection, with the above personal details and club record. Burnley 'A complete Record', Breedon Books, appears reference the same player but as Clifford Jones, with no Stephen as a lead Christian name, but, with the same previous clubs, as ENFA.

The reader may make what ever he thinks, but, the details do seem to infer that a C. Jones played at Burnley, and at the time period when the images were being collated for the card collection. There is no record concerning illegal payments invoving C.Jones or a connection with Rotherham Town and Chesterfield that might clarify.

An appendage to the record on ENFA entry for this player. (These can be added by anyone)

Family trees on Ancestry.com supports him being Samuel Clifford Jones, b. 13/12/1889 Rawmarsh (1891 census) and died 5/4/1959, Rotherham. Son of John Jones, a Rotherham Town FC Secretary/Manager. WW1 "guest" at Chesterfield Town FC, 1/1916 to cs 1917. Suspended from c/s 1917 to 8/8/19 in connection with illegal payments to players at that club. Selected for an England trial against Tottenham, 1921.
