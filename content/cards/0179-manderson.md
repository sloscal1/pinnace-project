## Biography

Bert Manderson was an Irish footballer who played as a right-back for Rangers and Ireland.

Following stints in Ireland with, Clifton, Dunedin, Macrory Memorial, and Cliftonville Olympic, Manderson, also played for Belfast Celtic from 1911 to 1914, then, Belfast Celtic and Glenavon, between 1913 and 1915, before joining Rangers in 1914, making his debut in a 1–1 draw with Aberdeen on 27 March 1915. He had joined Rangers from Glenavon for £150 ( £14,000+ now). He went on to make 370 league appearances (452 in total) in 12 seasons with the club,

He won seven Scottish league titles.

Manderson left Rangers to join Bradford Park Avenue* in 1927, making 40 appearances for the Yorkshire club.

He was also capped at international level by Ireland.

Manderson was trainer of Queen's Park from at least 1929 until his death.

*Research infers that he actually moved to Bradford City with Tommy Cairns, from Ranges, this seems incorrect, although Cairns did move to Bradford City in 1927 there is no trace of Manderson making the same journey, or ever playing at City.
