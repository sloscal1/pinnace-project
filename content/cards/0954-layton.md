## Biography

Between 1912 and 1914 Layton travelled across Australia, playing football for various small clubs. During this time he was 'capped' by Australia in a match between New South Wales and Queensland. Even though his normal position was full back he played the game as centre forward, scoring the only goal. Edward was the brother of William Layton (1875-1944), a footballer with Sheffield Wednesday.
