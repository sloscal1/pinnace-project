## Biography

He played in the 1925 Scottish Cup Final which Dundee lost 2-1 to Celtic.

Scotland 1 appearance v Wales 1-1 draw, 26th February, 1920, Ninian Park, Cardiff, Wales.

Posthumously inducted into the Dundee Hall of Fame, 2018.
