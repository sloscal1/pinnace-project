## Biography

Fast and enterprising winger who also played as an inside forward, turning out, 13 times for Blackpool as a guest during WW1, before signing for Blackburn Rovers in 1921 a fee of £1500 ( £61,000+ now), supposedly, changing hands, suggesting he was actually contracted to Blackpool. After 2 seasons with Rovers he moved to Stoke for the sum of £400 ( £19,000+ now) making 95 leaguer and cup appearances, scoring 6 goals. He moved to varying clubs before retiring in 1931.
