## Biography

Johnson is both Sheffield United's and Mansfield Town's all time top goal scorer.

His father, William Harrison Johnson, also known as Harry (1876-1940), also played for Sheffield United and gained six caps for England. His younger brother Tom (1911-1983) also played for the Blades for several years
