## Biography

It seems that in his early days despite being born in Poplar, thought to be London, his footballing career blossomed playing for clubs in Yorkshire and North Nottinghamshire, his affiliation to that area is unknown.

Stanton worked as a carpenter and joiner. He served as an Air Mechanic in the Royal Flying Corps (latterly the Royal Air Force) during the First World War, seemingly finding his self in London, during the war, as he actually played 82 games as a wartime guest at Brentford between 1916 and 1919 winning the London Combination league in 1918-19 season.
