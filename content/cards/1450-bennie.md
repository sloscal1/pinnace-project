## Biography

An intelligent inside forward or half back, having descended from a footballing family. His early career was spent in minor football before joining Parkhead FC.

Joining Airdrie in 1920 his career really blossomed drawing interest from teams south of the border, Airdrie being able to resist these approaches, as he blossomed into an International player, playing three times between 1925 and 1926, and representing the Scottish FA XI once in 1928. Retiring from the game in 1933 he became manager at Raith but success eluded him, resigning after three seasons.
