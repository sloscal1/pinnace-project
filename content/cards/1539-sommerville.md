## Biography

Sommerville was initially, understudy to Willie White, see card 257 on page 352 (of this book) and whilst playing junior football had performed as an outside right. On leaving Accies for Burnley and after refusing initial terms he joined former team mate, John Steel, see card 1541, (this page) at the Lancashire, club, perhaps not surprising, as both players were married to (same family) sisters.
