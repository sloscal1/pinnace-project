## Honours

## Biography

Jackson was generally preferred to rival Cyril Spiers( 04/041902-21/05/1967) for a first team place and featured on the losing side in the 1924 FA Cup Final, but was eventually replaced by Ben Olney (30/03/1943). He served with the Royal Northumberland Fusiliers during World War I and attended Durham University before taking up football full-time; his profession after leaving the game was a schoolteacher. Kidderminster Harriers
