## Biography

Born in Wombwell, near Barnsley in 1889 John was the archetypal defender of the period. As befitted a former miner he was a good tackler and cleared the ball with little finesse but was effective for all that.
Starting out at Worksop Town he joined Notts County just before the Great War but did not make his debut until after hostilities had ceased. He enjoyed a good first season at Meadow Lane but was unable to win a first team spot in his second, leading to a move to Luton in the summer of 1921.
After a reasonable first season he was then used mainly as a squad player at Kenilworth Road with his appearances cut back through a succession of injuries. Leaving Luton in 1924 he finished his career with three years at Hartlepools United.
John passed away in Loughborough in 1972.

Born in Dalry, Scotland on Boxing Day 1899, Bob came to Luton on trial in April 1921 and was awarded a contract due to the extra training he put in and perseverance shown.
For his first two seasons he learnt his trade mainly in the reserves but by 1923 he could not be overlooked and for the next seven years became a mainstay in the side. A left-half who went about his work quietly, he was only noticed when he was not in the side. A great reader of the game and sound tackler he was able to set up attacks using skill rather than leather the ball.
Given a free transfer in 1930, Bob went on to give service to Belfast Celtic.
