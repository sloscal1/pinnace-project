## Biography

An unassuming. unselfish and stylish Inside Forward, much revered and in 1925 much mourned.

He had represented Berkshire and Buckinghamshire at Football in 1908-09 season and much later represented the Southern League representative side.

An appendix operation kept out of the Southern League Championship side

His younger brother Tommy was a founding figure of the Watford Harriers Athletics Club.
