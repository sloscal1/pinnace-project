## Biography

Moss was initially trained as a joiner, but later worked at the Crewe locomotive works for the London and North Western Railway as a locomotive fitter, which he combined with playing football. He was skilled as a marksman and won many medals and silver spoons at shooting range competitions. During WW1 he served in the Royal Engineers and was wounded in France, being awarded the Croix de Guerre for an attack on a machine gun post.
