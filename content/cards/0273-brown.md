## Biography

* Referenced as loans but probably guesting club appearances during WW1.

He would on occasions captain the first 11 in league games.

He played for an unofficial 'Scotland' team in a tour of North America organised by Third Lanark in 1921.

'Craig Brown earned the man of the match award for his brilliant display in the opening match of Third Lanark select groundbreaking visit – the first foreign trip backed by the Scottish Football Association – which saw the team win 19 games out of 19 and score a total of 86 goals while conceding only eight'. Thanks to www.glasgowworld.com (2018).
