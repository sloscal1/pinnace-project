## Biography

Logan played as an inside right. He spent 17 years with Bradford City, making over 300 appearances and winning the 1911 FA Cup Final. he was the younger brother of footballer James Henry Logan, (1885-1961), who also played for Bradford City. After retiring as a player in April 1925, he worked as a coach at the club until October 1925. He then became licensee of the Girlington Hotel, Bradford.
