## Biography

Described as a strong and powerful inside forward, with a hard shot, as befitting the amateur lightweight boxing champion of Hertfordshire, thar he had been. Thanks to www.hattersheritage.co.uk

After retiring from football in 1926, Butcher returned to his family business of building artesian wells.
