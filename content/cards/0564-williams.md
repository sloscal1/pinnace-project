## Biography

Known as Rees, he started his football career at Merthyr before arriving at Wednesday in 1922, to solve the clubs positional problem at outside right, becoming a constant fixture in the Wednesday line ups.

Described as the best two footed player seen at Wednesday for years, he turned in some brilliant performances.

Small in stature and only 5' 5" tall, he made up for that lack of stature with his turn of speed and dribbling ability.

He reached his peak in 1925-26 season when he helped Wednesday win the Division two title.

In his following season he lost his place and subsequently transferred to Manchester United.

He gained the first 4 of his eight Welsh caps whilst at Wednesday, the remaining four, at United.

First appearance 12th February 1921 v Scotland a 2-1 defeat at Aberdeen and the last on the 17th November 1928 at Swansea v England a 3-2 defeat, he scored one goal for Wales v Ireland on the 9th April 1927 at Wrexham in a 2-2 draw. All games were in the British Home International Tournament.
