## Biography

A Southern League championship medal came his way within a year of joining Watford.

A clever Inside Right, eventually catching the eye of Nottingham Forest who paid an hefty fee, unfortunately he only played four times for the reds.

He scored Watford's first goal in the football league

It would seem he probably returned to Watford in some capacity as on leaving Watford in 1946 he was awarded £50 ( £1,700+ now) in recognition of his service.
