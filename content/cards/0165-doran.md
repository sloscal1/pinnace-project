## Biography

Newcastle Empire, Coventry City, Newcastle Utd, Norwich City.

1920-1924 Brighton & Hove 76 apps 45 goals

1922-1923 Manchester City 3 apps 1 goal

1923-1924 Crewe Alex 18 apps 1 goal

As can be seen, Doran was much a journeyman footballer, he later played between 1924 and 1927 for Mid Rhondda Utd, Fordsons, Boston Town, twice, and Shelbourne, but many of these could have been attached to other gainful employment.

Whilst at Brighton he represented Northern Ireland on three occasions without scoring between 1920 and 1923.

Doran went on to serve in the 17th Battalion of the Middlesex Regiment – the so-called Footballers' Battalion – and was awarded the Military Medal.

He was gassed at the Battle of the Somme & again at the Battle of Cambrai, being awarded the Military Medal while in France in October 1916.

In later life be became a publican in the North East of England, passing away in Sunderland.
