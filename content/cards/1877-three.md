## Biography

All were at Durham City between 1920 and 1924. I have worked out that the images for Durham were probably photographed in 1922 but it will be up to the reader which of these profiles the image is likely to be.

Albert Sidney Wilson

Winger

Durham City 1924-1925 2 apps 0 goals

His record is short, playing briefly for some junior Co. Durham teams but in or around 1922 he would have been in his late teens.

John Thomas Wilson

Wing Half

Durham City 1921-1922

This, I think is most likely to be the player pictured.

John had an illustrious career between 1914 and 1933, either side of his brief spell at Durham City, playing for Newcastle Utd in 1914 scoring 2 goals in 7 games, much later he played at Stockport County between 1922 and 1926 making 134 appearances scoring 12 goals.

1926 to 1932 he was an integral part of the Manchester Utd team of that period playing 140 games and scoring 3 times during his stay. His career seems to have petered out in 1933 after one season at Bristol City making 21 appearances and no goals.

Joseph 'Joe' Wilson

Winger

Durham City 1921-1923 49 apps 9 goals

He later played for Sheffield Wednesday 3 appearances, Manchester Utd no appearances, Norwich City 41 appearances scoring 4 goals and spent time at a handful of junior clubs.
