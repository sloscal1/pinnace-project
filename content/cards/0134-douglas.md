## Biography

Douglas made his reputation on the London Schoolboy circuit captaining the West Ham schools team to win the English schools shield in 1906. Between 1909 and 1911 Douglas played his early football with local church and junior sides, St Saviours (Forest Gate), Custom House, and later Ilford. At Ilford he played for England at amateur level.
