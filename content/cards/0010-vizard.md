## Biography

Wales 1911-1926, 22 apps 1 goal

During his time at Bolton, he appeared in the 1923, 1926 and 1929 FA Cup Finals, winning each time. He remained in the team until retiring in 1931 aged 41. He managed Swindon Town, Queens Park Rangers(London) & Wolves between 1933 and 1948.
